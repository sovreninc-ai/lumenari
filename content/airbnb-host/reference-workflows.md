# Reference Workflows — Airbnb / STR Host Pack

Real worked examples. Steal whichever ones fit your property. Names and details are placeholders.

## 1. Full listing description — 2BR mountain cabin

**Cover line (Airbnb title, 48 char):**

> Pine-clad 2BR cabin near Bow Valley trails

**Lead paragraph:**

> A quiet two-bedroom cabin on a residential street in Canmore, about 1.2km from the downtown core. Designed for couples or a small family who want to hike or ski during the day and have a real kitchen and a wood stove to come back to. Not flashy. Built to be comfortable for four people staying anywhere from a long weekend to a couple of weeks.

**The space:**

> Two bedrooms — one king, one queen — both with blackout curtains and good mattresses. One bathroom with a deep tub. Open kitchen and living room with a wood stove and a Yamaha record player. Hot tub on the covered back deck, year-round. Fast fibre internet (300 Mbps download, plenty for a stream-while-you-work scenario). The vibe is modern Scandi — a lot of wood, a lot of natural light.

**Amenities highlights:**
- WiFi: 300 Mbps fibre, mesh router for full coverage
- Kitchen: full size, gas stove, espresso machine + drip, basics for cooking
- Hot tub: 6-person, cleaned between stays, accessible year-round
- Wood stove: firewood + kindling provided in the box on the deck
- Parking: gravel pad on the right of the driveway, fits 2 vehicles
- Pet policy: dogs welcome with a $50 fee — please tell us before booking
- EV charging: 240V outlet in the garage if you have a portable charger

**Neighborhood:**

> Quiet residential street, 5 minute walk to the Bow River trail, 15 minute walk into downtown for coffee and groceries. Drive to Three Sisters trailhead: 8 minutes. Drive to Nakiska or Sunshine: 35-45 minutes. The Canmore Nordic Centre is a 10 minute drive.

**Things to know:**

> The driveway is steep — we plow it but recommend a vehicle with AWD or winter tires from November through April. Cell service is patchy in the back bedroom (WiFi calling works fine). There's one bathroom, which works great for two people or a family but is tighter for two unrelated couples. We don't have AC — Canmore is usually cool enough that you won't miss it, but if you're sensitive to heat in summer, the cabin can sit at 24-25°C on the warmest days.

Why it works: every section uses specifics. "Things to know" gets the tradeoffs out before booking. No "stunning" or "hidden gem." Reads like a person describing their place.

## 2. Guest message cadence — full sequence

**1. Inquiry reply (under 1 hour):**

```
Hi Sarah,

Yes, the cabin is open for the dates you asked about (Oct 18-22). Looks like you're traveling with one dog — happy to host them, the $50 pet fee will be added at booking. Quick question: any allergy concerns we should know about (we do have a couple of indoor plants and a wool rug in the living room)?

Once you book, I'll send a confirmation and the address. Pre-arrival details go out 48 hours before check-in.

— Mike
```

**2. Booking confirmation (sent right after booking):**

```
Hi Sarah,

You're confirmed for Oct 18-22 at the cabin. I'll send you the full address, door code, and arrival details 48 hours before check-in. In the meantime, if you have any questions about the neighborhood or what to pack (it's been cool here — bring layers), let me know.

Looking forward to having you.

— Mike
```

**3. 48-hour pre-arrival:**

```
Hi Sarah,

Quick details for your check-in on Saturday:

Address: 142 Cedar Crescent, Canmore
Door code: 4827 (works on the keypad to the left of the front door, push the # key after entering)
Parking: gravel pad on the right side of the driveway, fits two vehicles. The driveway is steep — AWD or winter tires recommended this time of year.
Arrival anytime after 4 PM. The house will be warm and the wood stove will be ready to light.

Wood and kindling are on the deck. The hot tub is heated and ready. WiFi network + password are on the fridge.

My number if you need anything: 555-XXX-XXXX. Looking forward to having you.

— Mike
```

**4. Check-in day morning (short):**

```
Hi Sarah — code 4827 is active starting at 4 PM. Safe travels. Text me if you hit any snags. — Mike
```

**5. Day-after check-in (most underused message):**

```
Hi Sarah,

You settled in OK? Let me know if anything's not working right — the espresso machine takes a minute to figure out, and the wood stove damper is sometimes sticky. Happy to walk you through anything.

— Mike
```

**6. Mid-stay (only if 4+ nights):**

```
Hi Sarah — hope the week's been good. Let us know if anything's come up. — Mike
```

**7. Check-out morning:**

```
Hi Sarah,

Checkout is 11 AM. No need to strip beds — please leave used towels in the bathtub, dishes in the dishwasher (start it if it's full), and lock the front door behind you (it auto-locks but a tug confirms).

Safe drive home.

— Mike
```

**8. Post-stay (within 24 hours of checkout):**

```
Hi Sarah,

Thanks for staying, and for treating the cabin well — Mark (our cleaner) noticed and asked me to pass along thanks. The dog photos on the IG account you tagged us in made my morning.

If the visit was a 5-star for you, a quick review when Airbnb prompts you helps us a lot. Either way, hope to host you again.

— Mike
```

## 4. Review responses — three at different ratings

**5-star response:**

> Sarah — glad the cabin worked for you and the dog. The hike up Ha Ling you mentioned is one of our favorites too, especially that time of year. Come back any time. — Mike

**3-star response (held overnight, posted next day):**

Original review: *"Cabin is cute but a few issues. Espresso machine was unintuitive and we couldn't figure it out. WiFi cut out twice during work-from-cabin time. One bathroom for a family of four was tight. Cute spot but not 5-star at this price."*

> Anna — thanks for the honest feedback. The espresso machine is a Breville with pods, and we have a quick how-to printed by the kitchen — I'll make it more obvious, since you're not the first to flag it. The WiFi drops you saw look like they correlate with two short ISP outages that morning (we've checked the logs) — we don't have a workaround for provider issues but we've credited you 10% of the night they happened separately. The one-bathroom limitation is real, and we mention it in the listing for that reason. Appreciate you taking the time. — Mike

**1-star response (held 24+ hours, posted carefully):**

Original review: *"Worst Airbnb experience. Owner unresponsive. Wifi was terrible. Place was not as described."*

> Mark — I want to make sure I understand what happened here. Looking back at our messages, I have you booked Oct 11-14 and you didn't reach out during the stay until 11 PM on the 13th asking about checkout time. We've checked the WiFi logs for your stay and the connection was active and at full speed the entire time. I'd like to talk about what you mean by "not as described" — please email mike@northcabin.ca if you'd like to resolve this directly. — Mike

Why it works: no defensive arguing for the 5- and 3-star. For the 1-star, Mike states facts (booking dates, response timeline, WiFi logs) without attacking — the next prospective guest reading this sees a host who's responsive and organized, not defensive. Held 24 hours so it's not the version Mike wrote at midnight.

## 5. Review of a guest (host writing about a guest)

When you check out a problem guest and you have to write the review:

**Example — a guest who broke something and didn't report it:**

> Mark stayed for three nights. Communication was minimal — required multiple follow-ups to confirm checkout time. Left late by 90 minutes without notice. Found a broken lamp in the second bedroom on cleaner walkthrough that was not reported. Will not host again.

**Example — a guest who left the place trashed:**

> Stayed four nights, group of four adults. Did not follow check-out instructions (dishes left dirty across the kitchen, bedding piled on the floor, full trash in the bins inside the house). Cleaner's turn time was extended significantly. Polite during stay but did not treat the home with care. Would not recommend for other hosts.

**Example — a guest who was fine but you wouldn't host again:**

> Sarah stayed four nights. Friendly throughout but several requests outside the normal scope (extra towels mid-stay despite a full closet of them, asked about late checkout day-of with no advance notice). Place was left tidy. Polite. Would not be a first-pick rebooking.

Why these work: factual, specific, useful signal for the next host. No "worst guest ever." No emotion. The next host reading this knows exactly what they're getting.

## 6. Inquiry reply — three patterns

**Confirm (straight booking question):**

```
Hi Jake, yes — Oct 18-22 is open. The cabin works well for your group of four. I'll send the address and the pre-arrival details 48 hours before check-in. Let me know if you have any questions on the neighborhood or what to bring (layers — it gets cool at night). — Mike
```

**Clarify (an ask that needs more info):**

```
Hi Megan, the cabin is open for those dates. Quick question — your message mentioned a "small gathering" with maybe 8-10 people. Just to clarify: the listing is for groups of up to 4 staying overnight, with up to 6 people total on the property at any time (this is the city bylaw for STRs in our area). If your gathering is a daytime visit with friends stopping by, that's fine. If it's a party with 10+ people staying late, we'd want to find you a different property that's licensed for that. Let me know what you have in mind. — Mike
```

**Decline (firm, brief):**

```
Hi — thanks for reaching out. The cabin isn't a fit for parties or events of that size, even daytime. Wishing you a great trip. — Mike
```

## 7. House rules that aren't theater

**Bad (theater):**

> ★ NO PARTIES OR EVENTS — RESPECT OUR HOME ★
> ★ NO SMOKING ANYWHERE ON THE PROPERTY ★
> ★ TREAT THIS HOME LIKE YOUR OWN ★

**Good (specific and enforceable):**

> - Max 4 guests overnight, max 6 people total on property at any time (per Canmore STR bylaw)
> - No smoking inside the cabin (deck and yard OK; please use the ashtray)
> - No parties or events. Family or friend daytime visits up to 6 people are fine.
> - Quiet hours 11 PM to 7 AM (neighbors will report noise complaints to the city)
> - Pets welcome with $50 fee — please don't leave them unattended in the cabin for more than 4 hours
> - Hot tub closes at 10 PM (noise carries to neighbors)

The good version gives the guest specifics they can actually follow. The bad version reads like a sign in a school bathroom.
