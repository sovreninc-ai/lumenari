## 1. Proposal — plated wedding (110 guests)

```
PROPOSAL — Smith / Chen Wedding — Saturday September 19, 2026

EVENT SUMMARY
A 110-guest plated wedding at the Cedarwood Tent Venue (40 minutes from our commissary), 6 PM
cocktails through midnight pack-out. The brief from the planner: rustic-modern, slightly formal
but warm, with the menu tracing what's good in Alberta in mid-September. Three asks built into
the menu — Hutterite goose, a vegetable-forward first course, and Saskatoon berry on the dessert.
Dietary list as confirmed at proposal stage: 8 vegan, 4 gluten-considerate, 1 severe tree nut
allergy.

PROPOSED MENU

Passed canapés (5:30 - 6:30 PM, cocktail hour):
- Heirloom tomato + whipped goat cheese, basil oil, sea salt (Vegan and GF-i versions)
- Beef tartare, mustard, capers, sourdough crisp (GF-i alt: served on cucumber)
- Smoked trout, dill creme fraiche, rye crisp (GF-i alt available)
- Roasted squash, walnut, sage, fried garlic (Vegan; nut-aware — we'll plate the nut-allergy guest's portion separately, walnut-free)

First course, plated at 7 PM:
- Roasted carrot + parsnip, hazelnut dukkah, herb yogurt, charred lemon, crisp grain (Vegan version
  with cashew yogurt, gluten-considerate version with quinoa) — vegetable-forward by design.
  Nut-allergy guest: hazelnut and cashew swapped for toasted seed dukkah and oat-milk yogurt.

Second course, plated at 7:45 PM:
- Hutterite goose breast, slow-roasted leg confit, beet-mostarda, charred greens, jus.
- Vegan main: charred king oyster mushroom "steak," lentil ragout, beet-mostarda, charred greens.
- Gluten-considerate: same plate, all components naturally without gluten.

Dessert, plated at 9 PM:
- Saskatoon berry galette, brown butter ice cream, vanilla bean cream.
- Vegan + GF version: Saskatoon berry compote, almond crumble, coconut cream (almond-free
  alternate for nut allergy: oat crumble).

Late night, 11 PM stationed:
- Soft pretzels with mustard
- Mini smoked brisket sandwiches on milk buns
- Vegan + GF alternates: roasted potato wedges with garlic aioli

DIETARY ACCOMMODATIONS

All courses prepared vegan or gluten-considerate with the dietary list provided. The nut-allergy
guest's plates will be flagged on every course and passed by name to the lead server. Our kitchen
is not certified celiac-safe, so gluten-considerate plates are made without gluten ingredients —
cross-contact is possible. We'll review the final dietary list with the planner 7 days before
event to confirm any changes.

SERVICE AND STAFFING

- Lead chef + 3 cooks on-site for 8 hours (3 PM setup through 11 PM service)
- Pack-out crew of 2 (11 PM to 12:30 AM)
- 8 servers on-site for 7 hours (5 PM through midnight) — 1:14 server-to-guest ratio
- 1 bar lead + 2 bartenders for 6 hours (5 PM through 11 PM)
- 1 captain on-site to interface with the planner throughout

LOGISTICS

- We bring our own mobile prep setup (we've done the Cedarwood venue 4 times — kitchen is
  adequate for finishing, not for full prep)
- Power: standard 120V outlets confirmed; we bring our own extension reels
- Water: venue has potable water, confirmed at site visit
- We will coordinate directly with the rental company (Cedar + Stone Rentals) for tables,
  linens, glassware, and china. Rentals invoiced separately, at cost + 10% coordination fee.

BEVERAGE HANDLING

Consumption bar. We provide bartenders, glassware, ice, mixers, garnish, and a curated beer/wine/
cocktail program. Client supplies alcohol direct to venue (our license allows us to serve, not to
sell). Pour-cost report provided post-event with the final invoice. Suggested SKU list will be
sent within 14 days of contract signature.

PRICING

Food, 110 confirmed guests:
- Canapés (4 selections, 6 pieces per guest): $32/person × 110 = $3,520
- First course: $24/person × 110 = $2,640
- Second course (mains): $58/person × 110 = $6,380
- Dessert: $18/person × 110 = $1,980
- Late night station: $14/person × 110 = $1,540
- Food subtotal: $16,060

Beverage service (consumption bar staffing only):
- Bar lead + 2 bartenders × 6 hours = $1,920
- Glassware rental coordination, ice, mixers, garnish: $480
- Beverage service subtotal: $2,400

Service charge (20% on food + beverage service): $3,692
GST (5% on food + beverage service): $923
Gratuity: at client discretion, paid separately to the team if offered

Travel (40 min each way, 1.5 hours of crew time + fuel): $420
Rental coordination (tables, linens, glassware, china): invoiced separately at cost + 10%

Estimated event total (food + service + bar staffing + service charge + GST + travel):
$23,495 CAD

Headline per-person (food only): $146/person
Per-person all-in (food + bar staffing + service + tax, excluding alcohol and rentals): $213/person

HEADCOUNT COMMITMENT

Final headcount due 10 days before event (Sept 9, 2026). Reductions of more than 5% within 10
days will be billed at 50% of the per-person rate for the reduction. Additions within 10 days
accommodated up to 5% with confirmation; over 5% requires re-confirmation of menu execution.

DIETARY LIST DEADLINE

Final dietary list (with severe allergies flagged) due 7 days before event (Sept 12, 2026). Late
additions of vegan or gluten-considerate plates can usually be accommodated. Late additions of
severe allergens may not be possible if sourcing or prep procedure can't be confirmed.

DEPOSIT + PAYMENT

- 35% non-refundable deposit ($8,225 CAD) due within 14 days of contract signature
- 50% balance due 30 days before event (Aug 20, 2026)
- Final balance based on actual headcount + service charge + GST + travel due 14 days post-event

CANCELLATION

- 90+ days before event: full deposit refund less $500 admin fee
- 60-89 days: 50% deposit refund
- 30-59 days: deposit non-refundable
- Under 30 days: 100% of contracted minimum due

NEXT STEPS

- Sign and return this proposal + contract by Friday May 23, 2026 to hold the date
- We currently have a second inquiry for this date and will release the hold if not confirmed by
  the deadline
- Tasting can be scheduled any Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon — $300/couple (4 dishes), credited
  to deposit on booking

Looking forward to it.

— Mike, owner-chef, North Range Catering
mike@northrangecatering.ca | 555-XXX-XXXX
```

Why it works: reflects back the planner's brief specifically (Hutterite goose, vegetable-forward, Saskatoon), names producers and techniques, dietary accommodation is honest about kitchen limits, pricing is itemized with no bundled hidden numbers, headcount and dietary deadlines are explicit, cancellation tiered, sign-back deadline real.

## 2. Proposal — drop-off corporate lunch (45 guests)

```

45-person drop-off lunch at Acme's downtown Calgary office, 12 PM lunch service. Format: build-
your-own grain bowl bar, plus a soup, a salad, and a dessert tray. Mid-priced corporate, focus
on dietary inclusion (you mentioned 6 vegan, 3 gluten-considerate, 1 severe nut allergy across
the team).

PROPOSED MENU

Build-your-own bowl bar (delivered hot + cold, labeled with allergens):
- Base: brown rice, quinoa, mixed greens
- Protein: harissa chicken, ginger-soy tofu (V/GF), slow-braised brisket
- Vegetables: roasted sweet potato (V/GF), grilled zucchini (V/GF), pickled red onion (V/GF), shaved
  cabbage (V/GF), charred corn salsa (V/GF)

Event total: $2,282 CAD

NEXT STEPS

Sign and return by Friday Sept 19, 2026. Standing offer at this price for any of your future
```

## 4. Dietary accommodation menu addendum

When a client sends a complex dietary list (often 14 days out), the operator drafts an addendum.

```
DIETARY ADDENDUM — Smith / Chen Wedding — Sept 19, 2026
Generated from confirmed guest list received Sept 9

Total guests: 108 (was 110, 2 cancellations)

Dietary breakdown:
- 8 vegan
- 4 gluten-considerate
- 1 severe tree nut allergy (Guest: Eleanor Davies, table 7)
- 2 dairy-free (new addition, was not on original list — accommodation confirmed)
- 1 pescatarian (added at the rehearsal)

ADJUSTED PLATING

Passed canapés:
- Vegan canapé portion confirmed for 8 + the pescatarian (she'll have V also).
- Eleanor's portion of the "roasted squash, walnut, sage" canapé will be plated with toasted
  pumpkin seed in place of walnut, on a separate tray marked.

First course (carrot + parsnip + dukkah):
- 8 vegan plates (cashew yogurt swap)
- 4 GF plates (no changes needed — naturally GF except the grain swap to quinoa)
- 2 dairy-free plates (coconut yogurt swap)
- Eleanor's plate: hazelnut-free dukkah (toasted sesame + seed mix instead), oat-milk yogurt
- Pescatarian: standard plate

Second course (Hutterite goose):
- 8 vegan plates (king oyster mushroom)
- 4 GF plates (no changes needed)
- 2 dairy-free plates (no changes — naturally DF)
- Eleanor: standard goose plate is naturally nut-free; will confirm with the line at fire
- Pescatarian: vegan plate (king oyster) — confirmed with the bride this is acceptable

Dessert (Saskatoon berry galette):
- 8 vegan + GF plates (almond crumble swapped to oat for the nut allergy table)
- 4 GF plates
- 2 dairy-free plates
- Eleanor: vegan + GF version with oat crumble (no almond)

KITCHEN PROTOCOL

- Eleanor's plates flagged with red tape on every course
- Lead server (Maria) will receive each of Eleanor's plates directly from the lead chef
- A laminated allergen card with Eleanor's needs will sit at table 7

This addendum to be signed by client and chef 7 days before event.

— Mike, lead chef
```

Why it works: this is the document that prevents the worst-case scenario. Specific guest names (in writing for the kitchen, not for the public-facing copy), explicit swaps per course, protocol for the line. The AI tends to want to generalize — make it specific.

## 7. Tricky inquiry — under-budget client

Inquiry: *"Hi, looking for catering for our wedding Aug 15, 2026, 95 guests, outdoor private venue. Our food budget is $80/person. Is that something you can work with?"*

Response:

```
Hi Sara,

Thanks for reaching out. $80/person can absolutely work — it shifts us from a plated dinner to
a different format, but a good format. Two options worth considering at that budget:

Option A — Family-style dinner. 3 sharing platters per table (one protein, one vegetable, one
grain or pasta), with a passed canapé hour before. Warmer feeling than buffet, still substantial.
Lands around $78-85/person depending on the protein.

Option B — Stationed event. 3 food stations + passed canapés. Guests move around, eat substantially
throughout the evening. Less formal, more social. Lands around $72-82/person.

Plated dinner starts around $125-145/person at our level — not because the food is fancier but
because the labor and prep is significantly different. I want to be straight with you: anyone
quoting you a 95-person plated dinner at $80/person is cutting something you don't want to find
out about on the day.

If A or B sounds like the right direction, I'm happy to send a proposal with menu options within
48 hours. If you'd rather see something else, let me know what you have in mind and I'll think on it.

— Mike, North Range Catering
```

Why it works: doesn't reject the budget, proposes realistic formats that match it, honestly explains why plated is more expensive (not just "out of budget"), warns about competitors who'll oversell at the budget, opens the door. No "we are passionate about creating unforgettable experiences within any budget."
