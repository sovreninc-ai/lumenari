- Stem count: 25-35 stems depending on variety

$195 + GST per week ($204.75 CAD all-in)

52 weeks × $204.75 = $10,647 CAD/year. Two skip weeks per year permitted at no charge (holiday closures); additional skip weeks billed at $50 cancellation/notice.

PAYMENT TERMS
- Net 15 invoicing, monthly
- 6-month rolling agreement, 30 days notice to cancel after initial 90 days

NEXT STEPS
- Sign + return for first delivery Monday May 5

- Monthly check-in for first 3 months, quarterly after
