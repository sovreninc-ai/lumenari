## 1. Spring open quote — new residential client

**Input shape:**
- Property: 6,500 sqft lot, single-family, established yard, central neighborhood
- Client: New, referred by neighbour, busy parents, low-touch communicators
- Scope: Spring cleanup, weekly mow May-Oct, two fert apps, fall cleanup, irrigation start/stop

**Output:**

```
Subject: Quote — Lawn care + spring cleanup, [address]

Hi [client first name],

Thanks for reaching out — [neighbour's first name] passed along your details. Here's a quote based on what you described and a quick look at the property on Google Earth.

SCOPE SUMMARY
Full spring cleanup to get the yard back to a fresh starting point, then weekly mowing May through October, two fertilizer applications timed for spring and late summer, fall cleanup, and irrigation turn-on/blowout. Quote valid 30 days.

INCLUDED PER VISIT (weekly, May-Oct):
- Mow all turf areas at 3" cut height
- String trim around fence line, trees, beds
- Edge driveway and walkways
- Blow clippings off hard surfaces

SPRING CLEANUP (one-time, scheduled late April-early May):
- Power rake / dethatch full lawn
- Core aerate full lawn
- Bag and haul thatch and debris
- First cut at proper height
- Edge bed lines

FERTILIZER APPLICATIONS (2x):
- Spring app (late May): balanced slow-release
- Late summer app (early September): higher potassium for root development
- Note: PMRA applicator licence required for restricted herbicides; we apply 4-way weed control only if you opt in separately

FALL CLEANUP (one-time, late October-mid November):
- Final cut
- Leaf removal and haul-away
- Perennial cutback if applicable
- Bed edge tidy

IRRIGATION:
- Spring turn-on and zone check (additional charge per visit if repairs needed — flagged in advance)
- Fall blowout with compressed air

SCHEDULE
Spring cleanup window: late April-early May, weather dependent
Weekly mow window: May to October, typically Tuesdays or Wednesdays on our route — we'll text you the day before if there's a change
Fall cleanup window: late October-mid November

PRICE
- Spring cleanup (one-time): [QUOTE: spring cleanup total]
- Weekly mow (per visit): [QUOTE: per-cut rate] x approximately 22 visits = [QUOTE: season mow total]
- Fertilizer apps (2x): [QUOTE: per-app rate] x 2 = [QUOTE: fert total]
- Fall cleanup (one-time): [QUOTE: fall cleanup total]
- Irrigation start/stop: [QUOTE: irrigation rate] each visit
Estimated season total: [QUOTE: combined seasonal total]

We can invoice monthly or per visit — let us know which works.

NOT INCLUDED
- Shrub pruning, tree work, mulch top-up (quoted separately if you want)
- Restricted herbicide application (requires opt-in and licence verification)
- Sprinkler repairs (we'll flag and quote any needed)
- Sod replacement for bare spots (separate quote)

TERMS
Payment net 15 from invoice. Reschedules due to heavy rain or thunderstorms are normal and pushed to the next available day on your route. Cancellation: 7 days written notice; no charge for missed visits if cancelled in advance.

Reply to confirm and we'll lock you in for spring.

Thanks,
[Owner first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Per-cut rate
- Spring/fall cleanup totals
- Fert app rate
- Irrigation start/stop rate
- Confirm 22 mow visits matches your route schedule
- Confirm 3" cut height matches your standard
- PMRA herbicide opt-in — confirm whether you offer this in your area
```

## 2. Design proposal — paver patio + planting bed

**Input shape:**
- Project: 240 sqft paver patio, retain existing fence, new 8x14 ft planting bed along south side
- Materials: Roman Stack or Holland paver in charcoal, cedar mulch beds, 12 perennials + 2 small ornamental trees
- Client: Existing client, repeat business, expects mid-range quality

**Output:**

```
PROPOSAL — Backyard patio + planting bed, [address]
Prepared for: [client first name + last name]
Date: [date]
Quote valid 30 days

OVERVIEW
A 240 sqft paver patio replacing the existing lawn area off the deck, plus a new 8x14 ft planting bed along the south fence line. Designed for low maintenance and four-season interest.

PHASE 1 — PATIO (240 SQFT)

What you see:
- 240 sqft of paver surface — Roman Stack or Holland in charcoal (you choose; samples will be brought)
- 6-inch concrete bullnose step from deck down to patio
- Patio bordered by 4-inch poly edge restraint, hidden
- Polymer sand swept into joints, stabilized with water set

What you don't see:
- Existing sod and 6 inches of topsoil excavated and hauled away
- 6 inches of 3/4" road crush base, compacted in two lifts with plate compactor
- 1 inch of bedding sand screed level
- Geotextile fabric between subgrade and base on the lower half (subgrade has clay layer noted on site visit)
- All sub-base sloped 1.5% away from house for drainage

What protects it:
- 2-year hardscape installation warranty on settlement and shifting beyond industry tolerance
- Polymer sand top-up included at end of year 2 (free service call, sand cost passed through)
- 1-year warranty on plant material under reasonable care

PHASE 2 — PLANTING BED (8' x 14')

What you see:
- Edged bed against south fence
- 12 perennials selected from a list including hosta, daylily, Russian sage, coneflower, sedum — final list confirmed at planting
- 2 small ornamental trees — likely Amur maple or columnar mountain ash (we'll confirm hardiness for your zone)
- 2-3 inches of cedar mulch top-dress

What you don't see:
- Sod stripped, soil amended with 2 yards of garden mix
- 4-inch poly edging around the bed perimeter
- Drip irrigation roughed in if you have an existing sprinkler system to tie into (separate quote if not)
- Landscape fabric NOT used in planting beds — fabric chokes perennials over time

What protects it:
- 1-year plant warranty on the trees (one replacement per tree if it fails in year one under normal care)
- Perennials carry a partial-season warranty (replace if dead before July of install year)
- Year-2 mulch top-up offered as a discounted service

INSTALL WINDOW
Best windows are early June or late August through mid-September. Spring install means living with mulch settling and a few weeks of perennials looking sparse before they fill in. We'll confirm date 2 weeks out depending on the spring rain.

WEATHER CAVEAT
Paver work cannot happen on saturated ground — the base won't compact properly and you'll get settling. If we hit a week of heavy rain, we push the install. We'll communicate clearly and reschedule to the next available window.

WHAT YOU PROVIDE
- Water access (outdoor tap or hose bib)
- Vehicle access to backyard (we'll need a path for a skid-steer and 1-2 trips with the dump trailer)
- Removal of any existing plantings you want preserved before we start
- Decisions on paver color, perennial selections, tree species (we'll send samples and a list)

WHAT WE PROVIDE
- All materials (delivered)
- All labour
- Permit coordination if required (low likelihood — patio is grade level, no encroachment on setbacks)
- Site cleanup daily
- Final walkthrough with you before sign-off

PRICE
Phase 1 (patio): [QUOTE: patio total]
Phase 2 (planting bed): [QUOTE: bed total]
Combined total: [QUOTE: combined total]

PAYMENT
- 30% deposit on contract signing
- 40% progress payment at sub-base compaction complete
- 30% balance on final walkthrough

Reply to move forward and we'll book the install window.

Thanks,
[Owner first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Patio and bed totals
- Confirm paver brand availability with your supplier
- Confirm 6" base depth matches your install spec for this zone (Alberta clay subgrade requires deeper in some cases)
- Confirm tree species hardiness for client's zone
- Verify drip irrigation feasibility on site
```

## 3. Snow contract scope — small commercial strip

```
SNOW REMOVAL AGREEMENT
[Owner business name]
Client: [client business name], [address]
Service period: November 1 to March 31 (extendable into April if needed)

TRIGGER DEPTH
Service initiated when accumulation reaches 1 inch (2.5 cm) on parking lot or walkways.

SERVICE WINDOW
Walkways and entrances cleared and salted by 7:00 AM on business days (Mon-Fri).
Parking lot plowed within 4 hours of snowfall ending during business hours.
Overnight events handled before 7:00 AM open.

INCLUDED AREAS
- Front parking lot (approx [sqft])
- Sidewalk along [street name] frontage
- 3 entryway pads (front, side, rear delivery)
- Stairs at rear delivery door

ICE MANAGEMENT
- Sand-salt blend applied at walkways on every service call
- Calcium chloride ice melt applied at entryways when temps drop below -10°C (sodium chloride ineffective below -7°C)
- Bare-pavement service NOT included — service maintains safe passage, not perfectly clear surfaces in extreme cold

PUSH LOCATION
Snow piled at the [northwest / specify] corner of the lot, against the fence line. If pile exceeds 4 ft, we'll discuss haul-away as a separate service.

PRICING MODEL
Per-event billing:
- [QUOTE: per-plow rate] per parking lot service call
- [QUOTE: per-walkway rate] per walkway service call
- [QUOTE: per-application rate] per ice melt application

Optional seasonal flat available: [QUOTE: seasonal flat] for unlimited service Nov 1-Mar 31. Caps client cost in heavy winter, caps our exposure in light winter.

FORCE MAJEURE
In events exceeding 30 cm of accumulation in 24 hours, service order follows priority schedule. Commercial clients with morning open times are first priority. Multiple subcontractors may be deployed. Communication will be by text to [client primary contact] by 5:00 AM if delays are expected.

INSURANCE
$2M CGL coverage in place. Certificate available on request.

INVOICING
Monthly, payable net 15.

TERMINATION
30 days written notice from either party. No early-termination penalty.

Signed:
[Owner] _________________________ Date: _______
[Client] _________________________ Date: _______

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Square footage of parking lot
- Direction of push location

- Confirm calcium chloride temperature threshold matches the product you stock
```
