# Quick Start — Instagram Creator Pack

You should be running in under 60 seconds. Pick your tool.

## Claude users

Open Claude. Create a new Project (Pro or Team plan for projects, but the prompt works in a regular chat too). In the project's "Custom instructions" field, paste the entire contents of `optimization-pack.md`. Upload `templates/pillar-and-caption-library.md` and `memory.md` to project knowledge. If you have 5-10 past captions you're proud of, plus your latest Instagram insights screenshot, upload those — they teach Claude your voice and your numbers faster than any description. Start a new conversation. First message: tell Claude your niche in one specific phrase, follower count, engagement rate, and voice in 2-3 adjectives. Then say what you need — "Define my content pillars" or "Rewrite this caption in storytelling style."

## ChatGPT users

Open ChatGPT. Click "Explore GPTs" → "Create a GPT" (Plus plan required). In the "Instructions" field, paste the entire contents of `custom-gpt-instructions.md`. In "Conversation starters," use the five listed near the bottom of that file. In "Knowledge," upload `templates/pillar-and-caption-library.md`, `memory.md`, and 5-10 of your past captions. Save the GPT (private to you is fine). Open it and start with: "Niche is X (specific phrase), I have Y followers, ER is Z%. Voice is [3 adjectives]. Today I need [artifact]."

If you don't have ChatGPT Plus, just paste `optimization-pack.md` at the top of a regular chat. You lose persistence and file uploads but the prompt still works.

## Gemini, Codex, Cursor, or any other AI tool

Open the tool. Start a new conversation. Paste the entire contents of `optimization-pack.md` as your first message. Add: "Acknowledge you've loaded this and ask me for niche, voice, what I sell, and the artifact."

For Gemini Gems: create a new Gem, paste `optimization-pack.md` into instructions, save, and use that Gem.

## Test it works

Once you've loaded the system prompt, paste this in:

```
Test run. Niche: the gap between therapy-speak and what actually helps overworked moms. 38k followers, 4.1% ER. Voice: warm but no-nonsense, occasionally dry, never preachy. What I sell: a $97 digital workbook called "When Coping Skills Aren't Cutting It" and brand partnerships with sleep and mental health brands. Artifact: define my 3-5 content pillars — I'll describe past posts after.
```

If you get back 3-5 named pillars, each with who-it's-for, what-kind-of-post, 5 example post ideas, and frequency — followed by a "Things to verify" block — the kit is loaded right. If it gave you pillars like "Wellness," "Self-Care," "Motivation," or any "Happy Monday!" energy, the system prompt didn't load. Paste it again.
