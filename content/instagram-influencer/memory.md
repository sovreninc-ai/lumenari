# Memory — Instagram Creator Pack

## Domain context

Instagram creators in the 10k-100k range live in a different economy than the micro-influencers below them and the macro-influencers above. They're past the "free product in exchange for a post" stage and into real paid partnerships, but they don't have an agent or a manager negotiating for them. Most are women in lifestyle, wellness, business, parenting, food, or design niches. The rhythm is something like: shoot batches every 2-3 weeks (one or two half-days of filming and photography), batch-write captions in 90-minute Sunday sessions, post 4-7 times a week across feed and Reels, post 5-15 story frames a day, and spend 30-90 minutes a day in DMs and comments.

The platform's economics: engagement rate (saves + shares + comments + likes / followers) is what brands measure. A 3-4% ER on a 30k-follower account is more valuable to most brands than a 1% ER on a 200k account. Brand partnership rates roughly track to $100-200 per 10k followers for a single feed post in lifestyle niches, more for high-conversion niches like beauty and finance. A full integrated campaign (feed + stories + Reel + usage rights) usually 3-5x that. Pitches that work lead with audience match and engagement; pitches that fail lead with follower count.

Success at this level looks like: 1-3 brand partnerships a month at real rates, a growing email list of true fans, an owned product line generating recurring revenue, and a save-rate trend going up (saves are the algorithm's strongest signal). Most creators who plateau plateau because their content lacks pillars — they post whatever felt good that week, the algorithm can't categorize them, and reach flatlines.

## Vocabulary the AI should know

- ER: engagement rate — (saves + shares + comments + likes) / followers, typically 1-6% for healthy accounts in this range
- Save rate: saves / views — the strongest single algorithm signal
- Share rate: shares / views — second strongest
- Reach: unique accounts that saw the post — different from impressions
- Impressions: total times the post was seen, including repeat views from the same account
- Followers vs. following ratio: a soft signal brands look at — 1:5+ is healthy in most niches
- Reels: short-form vertical video, currently the platform's growth engine
- Feed: the grid; the static or carousel posts
- Stories: 24-hour ephemeral content, the highest-frequency surface
- Highlights: saved stories pinned to the profile
- Link sticker: the in-story tappable link, the primary direct-traffic driver
- DM: direct message; brand outreach happens here for some niches, email for others
- UGC: user-generated content — when a creator makes content the brand uses, often without the creator being tagged
- Whitelisting: granting a brand permission to run paid ads from the creator's account
- Usage rights: contractual terms for how long and where a brand can repurpose the creator's content
- Allowlisting / Boosting: paid amplification of creator content by the brand
- Niche-locked: when the algorithm has firmly categorized the account and won't push outside that lane
- Carousel: a multi-image or multi-video feed post (up to 20 frames) — strong for saves
- Content pillars: 3-5 recurring themes that organize the account's content

## Common workflows

- **Content pillar definition**: trigger = creator feels scattered or wants to reset → steps = creator gives current niche, audience, and 10-15 past posts they're proud of → output = 3-5 named pillars, each with who-it's-for, what-kind-of-post, 5 example post ideas, and frequency
- **Caption rewrite (3 styles)**: trigger = creator has a draft or a photo and needs a caption → steps = creator gives the image/concept + which style (storytelling / educational / behind-scenes) → output = full caption matching the requested style, 60-200 words, with a hook line that earns the read
- **Story sequence for product launch**: trigger = creator launching a product or making a major announcement → steps = creator gives the offer, timeline, and existing audience context → output = 5-frame story sequence (Tension / Build / Turn / Proof / Land) with on-screen text suggestions and link sticker placement
- **Brand partnership pitch email**: trigger = creator has a brand in mind → steps = creator gives brand name, why they fit, a specific recent thing about the brand, and current metrics → output = personalized cold pitch with brand-specific opener, audience-match paragraph, conservative metrics block, specific deliverable proposal, and soft close

## What to avoid / common mistakes

- "Happy Monday!" / "Happy Friday!" openers — every variation reads as filler
- "Double tap if you agree" / "Drop a heart" as CTA — engagement-bait of a dead era
- Fake-vulnerable openers without earned specifics — "I'm finally ready to share..." about something low-stakes
- "Link in bio!!" with no specific reason to click — the click rate on these is functionally zero
- Bullet-list captions for every post — kills voice; fine occasionally, dead as a default
- Captions over 400 words for non-storytelling posts — most caption real estate beyond 200 words goes unread
- Content pillars defined too broadly ("wellness") to actually guide weekly content
- Brand pitches that lead with follower count — every brand checks the real numbers before replying
- "You guys..." as a caption opener — instant AI-tell in 2026
- Sequences of emojis as the entire caption opener

## Tone / register

A real working Instagram creator in this range sounds like someone with a specific point of view they've earned. Sentences are short to medium, sometimes one-line paragraphs for visual rhythm. They reference specific moments, dates, numbers, names — not abstractions. They write with one specific follower in mind, not "you guys." They use first person and direct address comfortably. They DON'T say "happy Monday," "you guys," "I just had to share," "I'm finally ready," or any variation of "double tap if you agree." They DO say "this is the part where," "here's what I learned," "I'm telling you," "the honest truth is." They write the way they'd describe the thing to one trusted friend who's also in the niche, not the way they'd present at a sponsored event.
