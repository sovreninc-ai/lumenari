# Hiring-Manager Intake — Internal Recruiter Pack

The intake meeting is the most important meeting of the search. 60-90 minutes with the hiring manager (and ideally HRBP) before any sourcing begins. If you skip it, the search will drift and you'll spend three weeks recovering.

This template walks the meeting and produces the notes the HM signs off on.

---

## Before the meeting — recruiter prep

- Read the JD draft (if any)
- Pull the prior incumbent's profile from the ATS if backfill (compensation, tenure, performance notes if accessible)
- Pull the org chart and identify the HM's manager, the team's peers, the HM's other current open reqs
- Check comp band status — is it approved? if not, what's pending?
- Identify the HRBP for this function; loop them in for the meeting if possible

---

## The intake meeting structure (90 min)

### 1. Business context (10 min)

Questions:
- Why now? What changed that this role exists?
- If we don't fill this in 60 days, what breaks?
- Is this a backfill or expansion? If backfill, why did the prior person leave specifically?
- What's the budget pressure on this seat? (Helps you understand exception authority later)
- What's already been tried? (If this is a re-open of a stale req)

### 2. Scope (15 min)

Questions:
- Walk me through what this person does day 1 to day 90
- What do they own at day 365?
- Reports to you. Reports of theirs? Dotted lines? Peer set?
- What three projects/workstreams are highest-priority for their first quarter?
- Who's on the team today and where are the gaps this person fills?

### 3. Must-haves (20 min) — the slowest, most important part

Questions:
- What does "good" look like at this level on your team specifically?
- Give me a story of someone who has done this job well. What did they do that I'd be able to spot in a recruiter screen?
- Now give me a story of someone who looked great on paper and didn't work out. What was the signal you missed?
- For each must-have you name, push: what observable behavior or proven experience would tell us they have it?
- Push back on keyword-based must-haves ("knows Kafka"). Translate to behavior ("has been on-call for a Kafka cluster and debugged a consumer-group rebalance issue at 3 AM")

The output: 4-6 must-haves, all written as observable behaviors or proven experience, NOT keywords.

### 4. Nice-to-haves (5 min)

Questions:
- What would be the cherry on top? 3-5 things.
- If you got 80% of the must-haves and 50% of the nice-to-haves, would you hire?
- (You're listening for whether the "nice-to-haves" are really hidden must-haves)

### 5. Deal-breakers (10 min)

Questions:
- What would make you say no on day 1 of a recruiter screen?
- What's the past-pattern signal that scares you?
- (Don't accept "low energy" or "bad communicator" — push to specifics: "can't articulate the why behind a technical decision" or "names personal life as the reason they left their last job")

### 6. Comp band confirmation (10 min)

Questions:
- What's the approved band? Base, variable, equity?
- What's the sign-on bonus tolerance?
- What's your exception authority? Mine?
- What's HRBP's read on flex if a strong candidate is above band?
- Has the band been benchmarked against current market in the last 6 months?

If band isn't approved, STOP. Get it approved before opening sourcing.

### 7. Sourcing approach (10 min)

Questions:
- Which companies have the profile we want? Tier 1, Tier 2.
- What companies do you NOT want me sourcing from? (No-poach, recent departures, others)
- Have any team members already identified a candidate? (Referral track)
- How public are we going with the brand? Hiring manager spotlight? Landing page refresh?
- What's the channel mix? Inbound JD posting, outbound sourcing, agency?

### 8. Loop design (5 min)

Questions:
- Recruiter screen, HM screen, technical/craft loop with [whom], final with [whom]
- Total candidate time?
- Target end-to-end timeline?
- Any candidate-friendly accommodation we offer by default? (Recorded loops, multi-day splits)

### 9. Scorecard (5 min)

Questions:
- For each must-have, which loop step assesses it?
- Who grades each step?
- What signal counts as "passed"?

The output: a one-page scorecard the panel will use at debrief.

### 10. The thing the HM didn't say (5 min) — final question

Question:
- "What's the part of this search that's making it hard that we haven't talked about yet?"

Listen carefully. The answer is often:
- "I've been burned by a recent hire and I'm worried about pattern-matching"
- "VP Eng is pressuring me to hire fast and I'm worried about lowering the bar"
- "I don't actually like one of the panelists I just listed but politically can't remove them"
- "The team is in a bad spot and I haven't told the candidate that"

Write this down. It's the most useful note in the document.

```

BUSINESS CONTEXT
[2-3 sentences]

SCOPE
First 90 days: [bullets]
First 12 months: [bullets]
Reporting: [direct manager, reports, peers]

MUST-HAVES (4-6, behavior-based)
1. [Must-have, behavioral]
2. ...

NICE-TO-HAVES (3-5)
1. ...

DEAL-BREAKERS
[Specific patterns]

COMP BAND
- Base: $___ to $___ ([approved by HRBP name, date])

- Equity: [#] / [%] / RSU value $___ at strike, [X]-year vest with [Y] cliff
- Sign-on tolerance: $___
- Exception authority: [recruiter / HM / HRBP] can flex [+/- X% / $X]

SOURCING APPROACH
- Target companies: [Tier 1, Tier 2]
- No-source: [companies, reasons]
- Channels: [posted, outreach, agency, referral]
- Volume: [target outreach per week, expected reply rate]
- Brand assets: [landing page, HM spotlight, recent blog post]

LOOP DESIGN
- [Steps with grader names and durations]
- Total candidate time: [hours]
- Target end-to-end: [days]

SCORECARD
| Must-have | Loop step | Grader | Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

THE THING THE HM DIDN'T SAY
[Recruiter's notes from the meeting. Honest. The HM may or may not want this in the signed-off version — discuss with them]

URGENCY

HM SIGN-OFF
[HM name, date of agreement]

RECRUITER CHECK-IN CADENCE
[weekly / bi-weekly, day/time]
```

---

## After the meeting — recruiter follow-up

- Send the intake notes draft within 24 hours
- HM reviews and signs off (or pushes back)
- Lock the notes in the ATS req file
- Use them as the anchor for every re-calibration conversation throughout the search

If the HM tries to change must-haves in week 4 ("scrap the on-call requirement, I just need someone smart"), walk them back to these notes. Don't change must-haves without a re-calibration meeting documented.

---

## What goes wrong in intake (and how to push back)

- **HM says "I'll know it when I see it"**: push for behaviors. "Walk me through what you'd see in the recruiter screen that would tell you we're close."
- **HM wants a unicorn**: gently surface tradeoffs. "Of these 9 must-haves, if we found someone with 6, would we hire?" If no, the bar is too high; recalibrate before sourcing
- **HM hasn't gotten comp band approval**: stop. "I can't open sourcing without an approved band — the conversation with candidates breaks without it. Let's get Marcus signed off, then I'll start outreach."
- **HM wants to skip intake**: hold the line. "I know we've worked together before. The intake is 60 minutes today to save us three weeks in the back half of the search."
- **HM is vague on deal-breakers**: ask for the past pattern. "Tell me about the worst hire you ever made for a role like this. What was the signal at the screen you should have caught?"

---

Things to verify before sending intake notes back for HM sign-off:
- Every must-have is observable behavior, not a keyword
- Comp band has an approval source named (HRBP name + date)
- Scorecard maps each must-have to a loop step and grader
- "The thing the HM didn't say" is captured (and the HM is OK with how it's written, or the recruiter holds it as private notes)
- Loop timing is realistic (panel availability checked)
- The notes are clear enough that a different recruiter could pick up the search if you got hit by a bus
