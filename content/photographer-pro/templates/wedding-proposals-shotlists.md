# Wedding Proposals + Shot Lists

Two of the highest-leverage documents a wedding photographer produces. The proposal wins the booking; the shot list saves the wedding day. Both are templated below with full worked examples.

---

## How to use this file

1. After you load `optimization-pack.md` in your AI tool, copy the prompt block for the document you need
2. Replace bracketed inputs with the couple's specifics
3. Paste into your AI tool
4. Compare against the worked example for the gut-check on voice and completeness

---

## 1. Wedding proposal — full template

### Prompt

```
Write a wedding photography proposal.

Couple:
- Names: [name + name]
- Wedding date: [date]
- Venue: [name + city]
- Approximate guest count: [#]
- Style/aesthetic in their words: [direct quote or paraphrase]

Coverage:
- Hours: [#]
- Number of photographers: [1 / 2 — solo vs second shooter]
- Engagement session: [included / add-on / not offered]
- Travel: [no travel / local / destination — include in pricing]

Deliverables:
- Edited image count: [400-600 / 600-800 / 800+]
- Online gallery: [Pixieset / Pic-Time / etc.]
- Album: [included / add-on / not offered]
- Prints: [included / add-on / not offered]

Timeline (per my contract):
- Sneak peek: [# images] within [days]
- Full gallery: within [weeks] — note any buffer I want to build into client-facing language

Pricing:
- Total: $[amount]
- Deposit: $[amount] / [%]
- Balance schedule: [terms]
- Tax: [if applicable]

Usage rights: personal use, standard.

Couple's decision driver:
- [what they're prioritizing — reliability, style match, recent reviews, second shooter coverage]
```

### Worked example output

**Inputs used:** Patel/Singh wedding, Saturday June 27 2026, 60 guests at family home in Bridgeland Calgary, "garden-grown, slightly wild, candid over posed." 10 hours coverage, 1 photographer + 1 second shooter, engagement session included, no travel. 700-900 edited images. Sneak peek 15 images in 1 week, full gallery in 6 weeks (buffer over my 4-week contract minimum). $4,200 CAD + GST, $2,100 deposit to book, balance 14 days before wedding.

## 2. Wedding day shot list

### Prompt

```
Build a phase-based wedding day shot list.

Couple:
- Names: [name + name]
- Wedding date: [date]
- Venue: [name + city]
- Ceremony location: [outdoor / indoor / religious / civil]
- Officiant restrictions: [any — e.g., no photo behind altar, no flash during ceremony]

Day-of timeline (rough):
- Getting ready start: [time]
- Ceremony: [time]
- Reception start: [time]
- Photographer's contracted hours: [start] – [end]
- Overtime rate: [$/hour]

Family formals (couple must supply):
- [Paste their list. If they haven't sent one, the AI should flag this as the first item to resolve]

Special moments per couple:
- [Anything specific — first look, surprise, religious tradition, deceased family acknowledgment]

Sensitivities:
- [Family politics, no-photo guests, etc.]

Style notes:
- [Candid-heavy / formal-heavy / specific aesthetic]
```

### Worked example output

**Inputs used:** Patel/Singh, June 27 2026 backyard wedding. Outdoor patio ceremony at family home. No officiant restrictions (friend ordained). Getting ready 11 AM at the Malcolm Hotel, ceremony 4 PM at home, reception start 6 PM. Contracted 12 PM – 10 PM (10 hours), overtime $300/hr. Family formal list supplied by couple (attached separately). Special moment: brief moment for Sarah's late mother at table 4 during dinner. Bride's brother is walking her in (not father). Aesthetic: candid-heavy, no posed group photos beyond family formals and wedding party.

**Output:**

## Common edits the AI will accept

When the draft comes back and you want to tune it:

- "Shorter — half the words, same content."
- "Make it more direct. Less explanation."
- "Add a line about [thing you forgot]."
- "Voice this as if I shot this couple's engagement already."
- "Strip every adjective that isn't doing work."
- "Tighten the overtime section — couple isn't price-sensitive."
- "More candid-emphasis in the reception section."

Each will produce a noticeably better second draft. The AI edits toward specifics better than it generates them.

---

## What to never let the AI generate

- The family formal list. Always from the couple. Always.
- Specific shots that depend on weather or compliance ("sunset photos at 8 PM" before sunset is confirmed)
- Officiant policies (varies by religion + jurisdiction + specific officiant — confirm at rehearsal)
- No-photo guest commitments (must be tracked from couple's direct guidance, not assumed)
