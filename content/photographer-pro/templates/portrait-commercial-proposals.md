# Portrait + Commercial Proposals

Portrait and commercial photography sell on different things than weddings do. Portrait clients buy experience and speed; commercial clients buy deliverables and usage. Below are templates and worked examples for both — including the explicit usage rights language that separates a commercial proposal from a portrait one.

---

## How to use this file

1. After you load `optimization-pack.md` in your AI tool, copy the prompt block for the genre you need
2. Replace bracketed inputs with the client's specifics
3. Paste into your AI tool
4. Compare against the worked example for voice and completeness — especially on the usage rights section for commercial work

---

## 1. Family portrait proposal

### Prompt

```
Write a family portrait session proposal.

Client:
- Family name: [name]
- Family size: [# adults, # kids with ages]
- Pets joining: [yes/no, type]
- Special considerations: [aging grandparent, recent loss, blended family, etc.]

Session:
- Date / window: [date or month/season]
- Location: [in-home / outdoor specific / studio]
- Duration: [60 / 90 minutes]

Deliverables:
- Edited image count: [40-60 / 60-80]
- Online gallery: [Pixieset / Pic-Time]
- Print/album options: [included / add-on]

Timeline (per my contract):
- Sneak peek: [# images] within [days]
- Full gallery: within [weeks] — note buffer

Pricing:
- Total: $[amount]
- Booking fee: $[amount]
- Balance: [terms]

Usage rights: personal use, standard.
```

### Worked example output

**Inputs used:** Holloway family — 2 parents, 3 kids ages 11, 8, 4. No pets. Grandmother (in her 80s) joining for one segment of the session, mobility concerns. Saturday morning in October, outdoor at Bowness Park Calgary. 90 minutes. 50 edited images. Sneak peek 8 images in 1 week, full gallery in 3 weeks. $650 CAD + GST. $200 booking fee. Balance day of session.

**Output:**

```
FAMILY PORTRAIT PROPOSAL — Holloway Family
Northbound Studio | Quote valid 30 days

WHAT YOU GET

- 90-minute outdoor portrait session at Bowness Park
- 50 edited images delivered in an online gallery
- High-resolution downloads for personal printing
- Print release for personal use (frame, ship to family, share on social — all standard)
- Grandmother's segment built into the first 20 minutes so she's photographed early while she's fresh

WHEN YOU GET IT

- Sneak peek: 8 images within 1 week
- Full gallery: within 3 weeks
- Online gallery active for 6 months; downloads available throughout

WHAT IT COSTS

- Family portrait session: $650
- GST (5%): $32.50
- TOTAL: $682.50 CAD

- $200 booking fee to confirm the date
- $482.50 balance due day of session (e-transfer or card)

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT

Personal use license: print, share, send to family, post on social. Standard.

If you'd like to license images for any business purpose (your company website, professional headshot use), that's a separate license — happy to quote it if it comes up later.

NEXT STEPS

- Confirm by [date] with $200 booking fee
- Session prep email (what to wear, where to meet, weather backup) goes out 1 week before the session
- One reschedule allowed at no charge for weather, illness, or family scheduling

A note on the grandmother's segment: we'll do her photos first while everyone's fresh. If she needs to sit down or step out for the rest of the session, the family group shots will still be done in the first 30 minutes so we capture them while she's there.

---
Things to verify before sending:
- Bowness Park location specifics — east side meadow vs west pond?
- Grandmother's mobility — can she walk 50m from parking, or do we need a closer location?
- Family's preference on candid vs posed split — I default 60/40 candid, fine to flex
- One reschedule policy — confirm matches my contract
```

## 2. Branding / headshot session proposal

### Prompt

```
Write a personal branding session proposal.

Client:
- Name + business: [name + business + role]
- Why they're shooting: [LinkedIn refresh / new website / book launch / speaker promo / etc.]
- Brand aesthetic / industry: [corporate / creative / fitness / wellness / tech / etc.]

Session:
- Date / window: [date or month]
- Location: [studio / their office / on-location]
- Duration: [60 / 90 / 120 minutes]
- Wardrobe changes: [# of looks]

Deliverables:
- Edited image count: [20-30 / 30-40]
- File formats: [web + high-res, sized variants if useful]
- Retouching: [included / add-on]

Timeline:
- Sneak peek: [# images] within [days]
- Full gallery: within [weeks]

Pricing:
- Total: $[amount]
- Deposit: $[amount]

Usage rights: business use (their personal brand) — clarify scope.
```

### Worked example output

**Inputs used:** Sarah Chen, founder of Resilient Coaching (executive coach). LinkedIn refresh + new website launch. Brand aesthetic: warm, approachable, professional but not corporate-stiff. 90 minutes at her downtown Calgary studio space + 20 min outdoor on the street. 2 wardrobe changes. 25 edited images, web + high-res. Standard retouching included. Sneak peek 5 images in 5 days, full gallery in 2 weeks. $850 CAD + GST. $400 deposit.

**Output:**

```
PERSONAL BRANDING SESSION PROPOSAL — Sarah Chen / Resilient Coaching
Northbound Studio | Quote valid 30 days

WHAT YOU GET

- 90-minute session: 70 min in your studio, 20 min outdoor on 17th Ave
- 2 wardrobe changes (planned at our pre-session call)
- 25 edited images
- Standard retouching included (skin smoothing, color, exposure, minor object removal)
- Files delivered in 3 formats: web-optimized (1500px), high-resolution (full), and LinkedIn-sized (square + landscape crops)
- Online gallery with download
- License to use the images for your business — your website, LinkedIn, marketing, speaker bios, social, email signatures

WHEN YOU GET IT

- Sneak peek: 5 images within 5 business days (so you have something to share if a deadline hits before the full gallery)
- Full gallery: within 2 weeks of the session
- Gallery active for 12 months

WHAT IT COSTS

- Personal branding session: $850
- GST (5%): $42.50
- TOTAL: $892.50 CAD

- $400 deposit to confirm the date
- $492.50 balance due day of session

WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT

Business use license for your personal brand: website, LinkedIn, marketing, speaker bios, social media, email signatures, podcast guest features, press kit, book cover (if applicable). All of it, for the life of your business.

What's not included: licensing to third parties (e.g., if a magazine wants to use one of your images in an article, they need to license separately through me). Republishing on a corporate employer's website if you ever go in-house at another company would need a separate conversation.

If you want a buyout (full image rights, including resale/sublicense rights), that's available — quote separately, typically 2-3x the session fee.

NEXT STEPS

- Confirm by [date] with $400 deposit
- 30-min pre-session call to plan looks, locations, and the specific shots you need for the website refresh
- Session day: arrive 10 min early to settle in, we'll work through the planned shots, and improvise around the ones that land

A note on retouching: standard retouching is what makes you look like you on a good day. If you want anything beyond that — significant skin work, slimming, scene composites — that's separate and we'd discuss in advance. For most personal brand work, standard does the job.

---
Things to verify before sending:
- 17th Ave outdoor portion — weather backup plan (covered nearby alley? indoor shot list extension?)
- Studio location — your space has good natural light from 11-2 confirmed? Or are we lighting?
- LinkedIn-sized crops — square + landscape is my default, want any others (banner, story)?
- "Lifetime of your business" language — confirm this matches your style. Some photographers prefer "5 years."
```

## 3. Commercial brand shoot proposal (with usage rights detail)

### Prompt

```
Write a commercial brand / lifestyle shoot proposal with explicit usage rights.

Client:
- Brand: [name + industry]
- Contact: [person + role + agency if applicable]
- Brief: [campaign or project description in 2-3 sentences]

Scope:
- Shoot days: [#]
- Pre-production hours: [#]
- Locations: [# + descriptions]
- Talent: [# models / # real people with releases / none]
- Wardrobe / props: [who supplies]
- Crew: [photographer only / asst / full crew]

Deliverables:
- Number of retouched final images: [#]
- Selects delivered within: [hours]
- Final delivery within: [days]
- File formats: [high-res JPEG, web-optimized, RAW if contracted]

Usage rights requested by client:
- Media: [web / paid social / OOH / print / broadcast / packaging]
- Territory: [regional / national / North America / global]
- Term: [months / years / perpetual]
- Exclusivity: [exclusive to client in category / non-exclusive]
- Talent releases: [included / client to provide]

Pricing:
- Creative fee: $[amount]
- Pre-production: $[amount]
- Retouching: $[amount or "included for X images"]
- Usage license: $[amount, priced separately]
- Travel + expenses: $[estimated or invoiced at cost]
```

### Worked example output

**Inputs used:** Hazelnut Coffee Co., 3-location specialty roaster. Project: lifestyle brand shoot. 1 shoot day (8 hours), 2 locations (main cafe + roastery), 4 pre-production hours, 2 talents (head roaster + regular customer), no agency, just the owner. 40 retouched final images. Selects within 48 hrs, final retouched within 10 business days. Usage: paid social + website + monthly email newsletter, North America, 18 months, non-exclusive in coffee category. Talent releases handled by me. Creative fee $2,800, usage license $1,200, retouching included for 40 images. Travel local, invoiced at cost.

**Output:**

```
COMMERCIAL BRAND SHOOT PROPOSAL — Hazelnut Coffee Co.
Northbound Studio | Quote valid 30 days

PROJECT
Lifestyle brand shoot for Hazelnut Coffee Co. Single shoot day across two locations — the main cafe (4th Ave) and the roastery (Manchester Industrial). Visual direction: warm, hands-on craft, real people in real environments. Final 40 retouched images for ongoing use in paid social, website, and monthly email marketing.

SCOPE

- 1 shoot day, 8 hours on site
- 4 hours pre-production (mood board, shot list, location scout coordination, talent release prep)
- 2 locations: cafe (morning, ~3 hrs) + roastery (afternoon, ~5 hrs)
- 2 talents: head roaster + regular customer (both pre-confirmed, releases handled by me)
- Wardrobe: talent supplies (their normal work attire); brand props supplied by Hazelnut
- Crew: solo photographer (me); no assistant required for this scope

DELIVERABLES

- Selects gallery (50-60 images, lightly edited proofs) within 48 hours of shoot
- 40 retouched final images within 10 business days
- File formats:
  - High-resolution JPEG (full size, sRGB) — print + OOH ready
  - Web-optimized (2000px longest edge, sRGB) — for digital use
  - Square + landscape + portrait crops on the hero 5 images for paid social variants
- Delivery via WeTransfer (full archive) + online gallery for ongoing access (12 months)
- Image metadata embedded with credit and usage scope

USAGE RIGHTS [DRAFT LANGUAGE — VERIFY WITH CONTRACT]

- Media: paid social (all platforms), Hazelnut's website (all pages, including homepage hero), monthly email newsletter
- Territory: North America (US + Canada)
- Term: 18 months from delivery date (extension available at 50% of original license fee per 12 months)
- Exclusivity: non-exclusive within the coffee/cafe/roastery category. Hazelnut has the right to use these images in the scope above; I retain the right to use the images for portfolio and self-promotion only (no licensing to competing coffee brands)
- Talent releases: I supply signed model releases from both talents covering the use scope above. Releases attached to final delivery.
- What's NOT included: OOH (billboards, transit), packaging (coffee bags, merch), broadcast (TV, paid video ads), licensing to third parties (e.g., if a coffee publication wants to use these). Any of those = separate license quote.
- Photographer retains: copyright (legal default), right to use selects for my portfolio + occasional editorial / press features. No use that would conflict with Hazelnut's brand.

This usage language is draft — make sure the contract matches. If you want a media lawyer to look at the binding version, I can recommend two who do photo licensing work in Calgary.

PRICING

- Creative fee (8-hour shoot day + 4 hrs pre-production): $2,800
- Retouching (40 images, included): $0
- Usage license (paid social + web + email, NA, 18 months, non-exclusive in coffee category): $1,200
- Travel + expenses: invoiced at cost, estimated $0-50 (local)
- SUBTOTAL: $4,000 - $4,050
- GST (5%): $200 - $202.50
- TOTAL: $4,200 - $4,252.50 CAD

PAYMENT TERMS

- 50% deposit ($2,100) to confirm shoot date
- Balance due net 15 from final delivery invoice
- Cancellation: within 14 days of shoot date, 50% deposit retained as cancellation fee; within 7 days, full deposit retained
- Reshoot policy: weather or talent illness, no charge; client-driven reshoot, day rate + 50% applies

NEXT STEPS

- Sign + return by [date] with 50% deposit
- Pre-production call within 5 business days of signing
- Mood board / shot list approval at least 5 business days before shoot
- Location walkthrough optional (recommended for the roastery)

ADD-ONS AVAILABLE

- Additional shoot day: $2,200 + usage extension TBD by scope
- OOH usage extension (transit / billboards): quoted on request — typically $1,500-3,000 depending on placement
- Packaging usage (coffee bag photography for retail): quoted on request — typically $2,000-4,000 for limited exclusivity
- Video — short-form lifestyle for paid social: quoted on request (referral to my video collaborator)

---
Things to verify before sending:
- Talent release scope — does the 18-month term match what the talents agreed to? Confirm with talents in writing
- Roastery location access — confirm with Hazelnut owner whether roastery requires food-safe protocols (boot covers, hair coverage on the floor)
- "Non-exclusive in coffee category" — confirm Hazelnut understands this means I can shoot another coffee brand (just won't license THESE images to another brand). Some clients want full category exclusivity for a premium
- GST on usage license — confirm CRA treatment of license fees for this size of business
- Cancellation policy — match my contract template
- Reshoot policy — confirm matches my contract
```

## Usage rights quick reference

A working photographer's cheat sheet. Use this to gut-check whether a proposal's usage rights section is doing its job.

| Dimension | Typical options | Pricing impact |
|---|---|---|
| Media | Web only / paid social / OOH / print / broadcast / packaging | Each tier ~25-50% pricing bump |
| Territory | Regional / national / North America / global | Global ~2-3x regional |
| Term | 6 months / 1 year / 2 years / 3 years / perpetual | Perpetual ~3-5x of 1 year |
| Exclusivity | Non-exclusive / exclusive in category / exclusive period | Exclusive in category ~50-100% premium |
| Buyout | Limited license / partial buyout / full buyout (all rights) | Full buyout ~3-5x creative fee |

The usage license is a separate line item from the creative fee. A $2,500 creative fee with limited web-only / 1-year / non-exclusive is fair. The same shoot with perpetual global all-media buyout is $10,000-$15,000+. Never let the AI conflate the two.

---

## What to never let the AI generate

- Usage rights language as "final" or "binding" — always flag for contract
- "Perpetual worldwide" or "buyout" terms in proposals without explicit photographer confirmation
- Talent release scope without verifying with talents
- Reshoot policy without matching the photographer's contract
- Crew costs / assistant fees without the photographer providing the rate
