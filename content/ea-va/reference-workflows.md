# Reference Workflows — EA / VA Pack

Worked examples for the things you write every day. Copy, adapt, send.

---

## 1. The soft no — worked examples

**Inbound: cold pitch for a 30-minute intro call**

```
Subject: re: Quick intro call?

Hi Derek,

Thanks for reaching out. Maya isn't taking new intro meetings right now — her calendar is locked through the quarter. If your pitch is timely, feel free to send a short summary by email and I'll get it in front of her.

Best,
M
```

**Inbound: warm-ish ask for "a quick 15 minutes" from a junior VP at a vendor**

```
Subject: re: Quick 15-minute coffee?

Hi Tom,

Thanks for the note and congrats on the new role. Maya won't be able to fit a coffee in the near term — she's running flat through the spring. Your team's main point of contact with us is still Priya, and she's a great place to route anything timely.

Best,
M
```

**Inbound: board member asking for 30 minutes "this week if at all possible"**

```
Subject: re: 30 min this week?

Hi James,

Thanks — Maya wants to make this work. She's tied up through Thursday but could do a 30-min call Friday between 1-3 PM ET, or first thing Monday morning at 8 AM ET. Let me know which works and I'll send the invite.

Best,
M
```

## 2. The firm no — when soft doesn't fit

For media requests, recruiter asks the principal explicitly doesn't want to entertain, or repeat senders:

```
Subject: re: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Maya isn't available for this. Thanks for understanding.

Best,
M
```

Four lines. No future opening. Doesn't apologize, doesn't explain.

## 3. The calendar request triage — worked example

**Inbound:** Cold email from a Series B founder asking for 30 minutes "to swap notes on scaling a CRO function."

**Triage:**

```
WHO: Sasha Reinhart, founder & CEO of Lattice (Series B, comp software). Cold — no prior contact. Political weight: 2/5 (peer founder, no immediate strategic value, low downside to passing).

WHAT THEY WANT: 30-minute virtual coffee. Theme: scaling a CRO function. Their preferred timing: "anytime in the next 2 weeks."

PRINCIPAL BANDWIDTH: No to this week / no to 2-week window. Could be a yes in 6-8 weeks if Maya feels generous toward peer founders that month. Default action: soft close, leave the door open.

DRAFT:

Subject: re: 30 min to swap notes?

Hi Sasha,

Thanks for the note. Maya's running flat through the next several weeks. If you want to send over the 1-2 questions you'd most want her take on, happy to flag them and see if she can write a short reply directly.

Best,
M
```

## 5. Inbox triage summary — worked example (end of day)

```
INBOX TRIAGE — MAY 14 — MAYA'S INBOX

REQUIRES YOUR EYES (4)
1. James (board chair): wants 30 min this week. Draft prepared offering Fri 1-3 PM or Mon 8 AM. Confirm before send.
2. Priya (head of CRO function): needs your decision on the regional structure proposal by Friday. Doc attached, summary inline.
3. Anika: personal — her father in hospital, says no rush, just wanted you to know.
4. Sarah from Latham: legal q on the term sheet, says non-urgent but flagged for visibility.

DRAFTED FOR REVIEW (6)
1. Sasha Reinhart (Lattice CEO): soft no, suggested he send 1-2 questions in writing. Draft above.
2. Derek Olsen (cold pitch, vendor): soft no, no follow-up offered.
3. Tom (junior VP at vendor): soft no, redirected to Priya.
4. Conference invite — Saastr asking you to keynote in Sept: declined politely, offered a pre-recorded video instead. Draft below.
5. Recruiter — looking for a CRO ref check on a former direct: brief 3-line response confirming dates + title only. Draft below.
6. Vendor RFP follow-up: handed to finance, holding cc'd reply for you.

DECLINED / DEFLECTED ON YOUR BEHALF (14)
- Speaking request — Toronto Tech Week
- Coffee pitch — series A founder, no warm intro
- Podcast invite — niche, low listenership
- 7x sales pitches (typical Tuesday)
- 3x recruiter "are you exploring" pings
- 1x media request (Fortune — kept the door open, draft on file)
- 1x board observer ask from a smaller investor — soft close

FYI ONLY (9)
- Newsletter digests
- Vendor product updates
- Two recurring monthly statements
- Office-of-CEO weekly rollups from leadership

---
I assumed:
- James gets the offered slots — confirm if you'd rather move them
- The Fortune door stays open
Confirm before send.
```

NEEDS YOU TOMORROW
- James (board chair) — pick a 30-min window: Fri 1-3 PM or Mon 8 AM

- Anika — personal note, no rush, but you'll want to reply tomorrow

TOMORROW'S CALENDAR
[8 AM - 6 PM compressed view, with the 3 meetings that matter most pulled out]

LATER THIS WEEK
- Board prep doc lands Thursday 5 PM
- Wednesday's Q&A: Priya's running it, you're on for 10 min open / 5 min close
