## 1. Recurring quote — new bi-weekly client

```
Subject: Quote — Bi-weekly cleaning for [address]

Hi [client first name],

Thanks for reaching out. Here's a quote for bi-weekly cleaning at the address you provided. Valid 30 days.

SERVICE TYPE
Bi-weekly recurring clean. First visit is billed at our deep-clean rate to bring the house to a baseline; from clean 2 onward, you drop to the recurring rate.

SCOPE PER VISIT (every two weeks)

Kitchen:
- Counters, stovetop, range hood exterior wiped
- Sink scrubbed and polished
- Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
- Appliance exteriors wiped
- Floor swept and mopped
- Garbage and recycling taken to bins

Bathrooms (full bath + powder room):
- Mirrors and glass
- Counters, sinks, faucets
- Toilets in and out
- Tub/shower surfaces scrubbed
- Floors swept and mopped
- Towels folded if left out

Bedrooms (3):
- Surfaces dusted (dressers, nightstands, window sills)
- Mirrors
- Floors vacuumed
- Beds made if linens left on top (we don't change sheets unless quoted)

Common areas:
- Living room surfaces dusted
- Floors vacuumed (including under cushions where accessible)
- Hard floors mopped
- Glass doors and switch plates spot-cleaned

NOT INCLUDED IN RECURRING (these are deep-clean items, available as add-ons or annual refresh):
- Inside oven, inside fridge, inside cabinets, inside microwave
- Baseboards (full)
- Blinds (full)
- Ceiling fans, light fixtures
- Grout in bathrooms
- Interior windows
- Wall washing

FIRST VISIT
First clean is billed at the deep-clean rate to bring the house to a recurring baseline. After that, the rate drops.

SCHEDULE
Bi-weekly on [day of week], between [time window]. We'll confirm the day and time before clean 1. We text the day before each visit.

PRICE
- First visit (deep clean): [QUOTE: first-visit rate]
- Recurring (every visit from #2 onward): [QUOTE: per-visit rate]
- Add-ons available — inside oven [QUOTE: rate], inside fridge [QUOTE: rate], windows [QUOTE: rate]

TERMS
Payment due day-of or within 7 days (e-transfer, credit card on file, or invoice — your choice).
Cancellation: 24 hours notice or 50% lockout fee if we arrive to a locked door.
Pets: please tell us about pet routines (we love dogs, just want to know who we're meeting).
Key/access: we can use a lockbox code, garage code, or you can let us in — let us know your preference.

INSURANCE
$2M CGL coverage in place — certificate on request.
Our cleaners are covered by [WCB / WSIB / state workers' comp].
We carry a [QUOTE: bond amount] janitorial bond.

Reply to confirm and we'll book your first clean.

Thanks,
[Owner first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- First-visit and recurring rates
- Add-on rates for oven, fridge, windows
- Confirm bond amount
- Confirm day-of-week and time window for the route
- Confirm whether bed linens are included if left out (varies by operator)
```

## 4. Training one-pager — new cleaner, day 1

```
JOB: 3-bed 2-bath family home, bi-weekly recurring, [neighbourhood]
TIME ALLOTTED: 3 hours

PRODUCTS YOU'LL USE
- Blue bottle (glass cleaner) — mirrors, glass, stainless. Lives in the front pouch of the caddy.
- Green bottle (all-purpose) — counters, surfaces, switch plates. Lives in the front pouch.
- White bottle (bathroom cleaner) — tubs, showers, toilets. Lives in the back pouch — keep separate from kitchen products.
- Microfiber cloths — colour-coded: yellow for kitchen, red for bathrooms, blue for glass, green for surfaces. Do not cross-use.
- Vacuum + extension hose. Mop with microfiber pads. Replace pads between bathrooms and floors.

ROOM-BY-ROOM (do them in this order)

1. Kitchen (35 minutes)
   - Counters cleared and wiped (move small items, wipe under, replace)
   - Stovetop and range hood
   - Sink scrubbed, faucet polished
   - Appliance exteriors
   - Floor — sweep first, then mop
   - Garbage out to bin

2. Bathroom #1 — main (25 min)
   - Mirror first
   - Counter, sink, faucet
   - Toilet — outside, then inside, then base
   - Tub/shower — wet, scrub, rinse
   - Floor last

3. Bathroom #2 — powder (15 min) — same order, faster, no shower

4. Bedrooms (10 min each)
   - Dust surfaces top to bottom
   - Mirrors
   - Vacuum
   - Beds made only if linens left on top (no sheet changes unless owner specifies)

5. Common areas (30 min)
   - Living room surfaces
   - Glass doors
   - Vacuum entire main floor (including under cushions where reachable)
   - Mop hard floors last

WHAT NOT TO DO
- Don't use bleach on granite or marble counters
- Don't move framed art or family photos
- Don't unplug anything (the WiFi router, the alarm panel)
- Don't let the dog out of the basement (note on door)
- Don't open the office door (client's home office, marked "no clean")

WHAT TO DO IF SOMETHING BREAKS OR GOES WRONG
- Stop. Don't try to fix it.
- Don't tell the client anything beyond "I'll have to check with my owner."
- Photograph the issue (and what was around it).
- Text [owner first name]: [owner phone]. Wait for instructions.

BEFORE YOU LEAVE (5 checks)
1. All windows closed if you opened any
2. All lights off
3. Dog accounted for (in basement, door closed)
4. Front door locked behind you with the code
5. Photo of finished kitchen + finished main bathroom sent to dispatch

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Confirm product brands and bottle colour codes match your kit
- Confirm cleaner pay rate (not on the doc; goes on the schedule)
- Confirm dog policy on this property (varies)
```
