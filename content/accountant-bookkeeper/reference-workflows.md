# Reference Workflows — Accountant + Bookkeeper Pack

Five worked templates for the documents you write the most. Copy and adapt.

---

## 1. Client intake checklist (new SMB engagement)

Send this as a follow-up after the discovery call. Don't dump every field on the prospect at once — three sections, each with a clear ask.

```
SUBJECT: Welcome — getting you set up

Hi [first name],

Glad we're working together. To get your books on a clean footing, I need three rounds of info. Below is round 1 — the rest comes after we have access to your software and bank feeds.

ROUND 1 — ENTITY AND PRIOR YEAR
- Entity type: [sole prop / partnership / LLC / S-corp / C-corp / CCPC]
- Federal EIN / Business Number
- State / provincial registrations (sales tax, payroll, workers' comp)
- Fiscal year-end
- Most recent filed tax return (PDF)
- Prior-year financial statements (P&L and BS) — even if rough
- Prior accountant or bookkeeper name and contact (we'll need a release)

ROUND 1 — SOFTWARE AND BANK ACCESS
- Current accounting software (QBO / Xero / Sage / Wave / spreadsheets)
- Will I be added as accountant user or transferred ownership? (We can advise.)
- Bank accounts and credit cards — list all that the business uses
- Payment processors (Stripe / Square / Shopify Payments / etc.)
- Payroll system if separate

ROUND 1 — OPERATIONS
- Who else touches the books? (You, your spouse, an admin, nobody)
- Document collection — what's your current habit? (Receipts in a shoebox, Dext, Hubdoc, nothing)
- Volume estimate — transactions per month, employees, contractors

Once I have this I'll send round 2 (chart of accounts review and prior-year cleanup scope).

Note: anything I send is documentation, not tax or legal advice. For tax positions and filing decisions, your credentialed accountant or attorney has final say.

[First name]
[Firm]
```

## 2. Monthly close summary email — worked example

Client: 9-employee residential remodeling contractor in Colorado. CCPC equivalent: S-corp. QBO + Hubdoc + Gusto.

```
SUBJECT: Northern Sky Builders — April close summary

April books are closed. Here's what stood out.

THE NUMBERS
- Revenue: $284,600 (down 8% MoM, up 14% YoY)
- Gross margin: $89,200 / 31.3% (down from 34.1% in March)
- OpEx: $52,400 (in line with prior 3 months)
- Net income: $36,800
- Cash at month-end: $112,300

WHAT MOVED AND WHY
Revenue dipped because the Henderson kitchen job billed in March, not April — a timing item, not a slowdown. Margin tightened 2.8 points because the Westside addition ran $7,400 over the budgeted material allowance, mostly cabinetry. Worth a quick conversation on whether to push some of that back to the client per the change-order clause.

WHAT I'D WATCH
AR aging is creeping. $38,400 over 60 days, all from one GC (Cascade Construction) on the office remodel. That's the third month they've slid. Worth a direct conversation before it hits 90.

QUESTIONS FOR YOU
- I coded the $4,200 Home Depot run on April 14 to COGS — confirm that was for the Westside job and not stock for the truck.
- The $1,800 Mountain Property Management charge — owner draw or business expense? (If it's the duplex you mentioned, we should set up a separate class.)

NEXT STEPS
Q2 sales tax filing is due July 20 — I'll have the working papers to you by July 12. Quarterly tax check-in with your CPA: I'll loop them in early June with the Q1+Q2 package.

Talk soon,
Dana
Hartline Bookkeeping

---
Not tax or legal advice. For tax positions or filing decisions, your credentialed accountant or attorney has final say.
```

## 3. AR dunning sequence — worked example

Same client. Cascade Construction owes $38,400 across three invoices, oldest 73 days.

```
DAY 7 — friendly
SUBJECT: Invoice 1042 — quick check
Hi Marcus, just making sure invoice 1042 ($12,800, due May 4) didn't get lost. Let me know if you need it resent or if there's a hold-up on the AP side. Thanks — Dana.

DAY 15 — firmer
SUBJECT: Invoice 1042 — past due
Hi Marcus, invoice 1042 for $12,800 is now 11 days past due. Per the master services agreement, terms are net 30. If there's an issue on this one or on 1038 and 1040, happy to hop on a call — easier to sort live than email. Otherwise can we plan on payment by [date 7 days out]?

DAY 30 — direct
SUBJECT: Invoices 1038, 1040, 1042 — 30 days past
Marcus, the three open invoices total $38,400, oldest 73 days. The MSA late-fee clause activates at 30 days at 1.5%/month — I'm not applying it yet, but I want to flag we're at the line. What's the right next step from your side? If cash flow's the issue, we can talk about a payment plan. If something's contested on a line item, let me know which one and I'll pull the documentation.

DAY 45 — escalation
SUBJECT: Past-due balance — pausing work
Marcus, per our engagement letter, I'm pausing new work on the Carter and Penn jobs until the $38,400 balance is settled or we have a payment plan in writing. This isn't personal — it's the clause I have to enforce so the firm can keep doing right by our other clients. I'd like to talk this week. Best times for me are Wed 10am or Thu 2pm Mountain.

---
Not tax or legal advice. For tax positions or filing decisions, your credentialed accountant or attorney has final say.
```

## 4. Engagement letter clauses — three worked examples

Each clause flagged for counsel review. Plain English first; the formal version is for the lawyer to bless.

```
SCOPE OF SERVICES (BOOKKEEPING ONLY)

We will:
- Reconcile your business bank accounts and credit cards monthly, within 10 business days of month-end
- Code transactions to the chart of accounts we mutually agree on
- Run monthly P&L and balance sheet reports
- Prepare quarter-end and year-end packages for your tax preparer
- Manage AR aging and produce a weekly AR report
- Prepare and file GST/HST or sales tax returns where included in scope

We will NOT:
- Prepare or sign your federal or provincial income tax return
- Provide tax advice or take positions on deductibility, classification, or filing
- Provide audit, review, or other assurance services
- Provide legal advice or interpret contracts

If you ask a question outside the scope above, we'll route it to your credentialed accountant or attorney.

[CONFIRM: scope items above match what was quoted in the proposal]
Verify with your professional liability carrier and a business attorney for [state/province] before relying on this language.

---

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Our total liability to you for any claim arising from our services, regardless of cause or theory of liability, is limited to the fees you have paid us in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

This limitation does not apply to gross negligence, willful misconduct, or fraud.

[CONFIRM: 12-month cap matches what's quoted; some jurisdictions and some PL carriers prefer a flat-dollar cap or a multiple of the engagement fee]
Verify with your professional liability carrier and a business attorney for [state/province] before relying on this language.

---

TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this engagement on 30 days' written notice. Fees earned through the termination date are due within 14 days. Upon termination:
- We will provide you with a packaged copy of your accounting file
- We will cooperate reasonably with a successor bookkeeper or accountant
- You retain ownership of your accounting data; we retain our working papers for [6/7] years per our document retention policy

If fees are unpaid more than 45 days past due, we may suspend services on 10 days' notice.

[CONFIRM: notice period, retention period (CRA expects 6, IRS varies), file ownership language matches your QBO/Xero setup]
Verify with your professional liability carrier and a business attorney for [state/province] before relying on this language.

---
Not tax or legal advice. For tax positions or filing decisions, your credentialed accountant or attorney has final say.
```

## 5. Advisory upsell — soft surface + direct ask

```
SOFT SURFACE (embedded in a regular close email)

WHAT I'D WATCH
Your cash conversion cycle has stretched from 32 days to 47 over the last quarter — partly the AR aging, partly the new vendor terms with Pacific Supply. Happy to map the working-capital impact if you want a clearer picture going into Q3. No charge for the first conversation.

---

DIRECT ASK (separate email, after 2-3 soft surfaces)

SUBJECT: A conversation about advisory work

Hi Sara,

Over the last three months you've asked me about hiring a second project manager, refinancing the truck loan, and whether to raise prices for the commercial line. Those are all good questions — and they're outside what bookkeeping covers.

I want to suggest we add an advisory tier to your engagement. Here's what it'd look like:

- Monthly 45-minute working session, scheduled, agenda set by you
- Quarterly cash-flow forecast with three scenarios
- AR strategy review (which we'd benefit from now)
- Pricing review at year-end and mid-year
- Direct line for ad-hoc questions (within reason — defined in the new SOW)

Fixed fee: $1,800/month, no hourly creep. That's on top of the current $650/month bookkeeping engagement.

Want to set up a 20-minute call to talk through it? No pressure either way — I'll keep doing the bookkeeping work the same way regardless.

[First name]

---
Not tax or legal advice. For tax positions or filing decisions, your credentialed accountant or attorney has final say.
```
