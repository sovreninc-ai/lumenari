# Reference Workflows — Handyman Pack

Worked examples. Prices are placeholders — plug your numbers.

## 1. Text-message reply — Facebook lead

**Customer message:**
"Hi, hoping you can help. Need someone to hang a 65-inch TV on the living room wall, mount the bracket for me, hide the cables in a wall channel, and put up two floating shelves on either side. Could you do this Saturday morning?"

**Reply:**

```
Hey [customer first name] — yeah, Saturday morning works for me. Couple quick questions:

- Do you have the TV bracket already, or should I bring one?
- The floating shelves — are those yours too, or do you need me to grab them at the hardware store on the way?
- The wall a regular drywall stud wall, or is it brick or concrete? (Different anchors.)

Ballpark: TV mount + cable concealment + 2 shelves is usually about 2-2.5 hours on-site. Quote with my own materials [QUOTE: total with materials]; if you have everything, [QUOTE: labour only].

I've got 9-11 AM open Saturday. Text yes and I'll lock it in.

Thanks,
[Handyman first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Total with materials
- Labour-only rate
- Confirm 2-hour Saturday window matches your route plan
- Bracket selection if you're providing — standard tilting mount for 65" is typical, fixed if budget
```

## 2. Half-day quote — interior punch list

**Job:** Customer is moving out of a rental and the landlord's punch list has 11 small items: 3 nail holes in living room, 2 dings in kitchen wall, sticking bedroom door, loose bathroom towel bar, replace broken closet rod, paint touch-up in hallway, replace one cracked outlet cover, fix toilet flapper, re-caulk kitchen sink edge, replace one light bulb in bathroom fixture (also needs cleaning), tighten loose stair handrail bracket.

**Quote:**

```
Subject: Move-out punch list quote — [address]

Hi [customer first name],

Here's the quote for the move-out punch list. Should be a half-day job — 4 hours on-site if everything goes the way I expect.

SCOPE
1. 3 nail holes in living room — patch, sand, touch-up paint (need to know your wall paint colour / pickup)
2. 2 dings in kitchen wall — same patch + touch-up
3. Sticking bedroom door — likely planing the latch side or re-aligning hinges
4. Loose bathroom towel bar — pull, re-anchor properly, re-mount
5. Broken closet rod — replace with similar (bring a standard rod + brackets)
6. Hallway paint touch-up where flagged
7. Cracked outlet cover replacement (cover only — not the receptacle)
8. Toilet flapper replacement
9. Re-caulk kitchen sink edge
10. Bathroom fixture — replace bulb + clean glass shade
11. Loose stair handrail — tighten or re-anchor bracket

TIME
Half-day on-site, approximately 4 hours.

MATERIALS
- Patching compound, caulk, drywall anchors, paint touch-up supplies, replacement closet rod, outlet cover, toilet flapper — I'll bring all of this
- Wall paint touch-up: I need a sample of your wall colour. If you've got the leftover can, perfect. If not, I can do a colour match at the paint store — adds [QUOTE: paint match] and 15 min on the way

PRICE
Labour (half-day): [QUOTE: half-day rate]
Materials: [QUOTE: materials estimate]
Total: [QUOTE: total]
Paint match if needed: [QUOTE: paint match add-on]

SCOPE NOTE
Quote covers the items above. If you spot anything else during the visit, I'm happy to look at it — add-ons get priced on the spot before I start. If add-ons push the day too long, I'll schedule them for a separate visit so I don't run late on my next customer.

SCHEDULE
Available [day] morning or [day] afternoon. Arrival window will be 2 hours wide — I'll text the day before with the firm window.

PAYMENT
E-transfer or cash on completion. I'll leave you a paper invoice for the landlord/deposit return.

Reply yes (or with questions) and I'll lock it in.

Thanks,
[Handyman first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Half-day rate
- Materials estimate (rough — covers patching/caulk/anchors/rod/cover/flapper)
- Paint match add-on rate
- Confirm available days
- Confirm whether the toilet flapper is a standard fit or a specialty (some toilets need specific parts)
```

## 5. Referral-out script — panel upgrade request

**Customer message:**
"Hey, hoping you can help — my home inspector flagged that my electrical panel is the original 60-amp from 1972 and needs to be upgraded to a 100-amp or 200-amp. Can you do that?"

**Reply:**

```
Hey [customer first name], thanks for thinking of me. That one's outside what I'm allowed to do as a handyman — anything in the electrical panel needs a licensed electrician because of code requirements, the utility coordination for the meter pull, and the permit.

The guy I'd send you to is [electrician first name + business name]. I've worked alongside him on a couple of houses and he's fair, he shows up, and he doesn't try to sell you stuff you don't need. His number is [number].

If you've got anything else around the property — replacing the fixtures the new panel will be feeding, the painting around the panel after they cut and patch the drywall, drywall patching from the work itself — that's the kind of thing I can do. Reach back out once you know your install timeline and I can coordinate the cleanup work on the back end.

Thanks,
[Handyman first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Confirm the electrician contact name and number
- Adjust the explanation to your jurisdiction (e.g., utility involvement, permit specifics vary)
```
