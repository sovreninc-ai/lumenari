# Sponsor Deck + Cold Pitch Templates

Three cold-pitch templates by sponsor category, a sponsor deck outline, and a follow-up cadence. All with worked examples.

The pitches assume the streamer has a media kit or analytics screenshots ready. The pitch is the cold open; the deck closes the deal.

---

## The sponsor deck outline

If a brand replies positively, you need a 5-7 page deck or PDF ready inside 24 hours. Default structure:

**Page 1 — Who I am (1 page)**
- Streamer handle, photo / headshot or stream still
- One-sentence identity ("Apex ranked grinder who narrates every fight like a sports broadcaster")
- Years streaming, partner/affiliate status
- 1-2 line audience summary

**Page 2 — The audience (1 page)**
- Average CCV (peak + total stream average — be honest about the difference)
- Stream cadence: nights per week, hours per stream
- Follower count + sub count + Discord member count
- Geography breakdown (top 3-5 countries by share)
- Age/gender skew if available from analytics
- Engagement: chat messages per hour, unique chatters per stream

**Page 3 — Why this brand fits (1 page)**
- The specific audience-product match in 2-3 sentences
- Why this fits THIS brand specifically (not why it fits "any peripherals company")

**Page 4 — Past sponsor work (1 page, only if you have it)**
- Brand logos with permission
- A specific example outcome (CTR on a code, conversion to a trial, etc.) — anonymized if needed
- Skip this page if you don't have data; don't fabricate

**Page 5 — Placement options (1-2 pages)**
- Tiered options: a 1-stream pilot, a 3-stream pack, a 6-stream pack
- For each: what's included (logo overlay placement, verbal mention frequency, dedicated segment length, social cross-post, Discord pinned post)
- CPM or flat-rate per tier
- What's NOT included (so you don't get nickel-and-dimed)

**Page 6 — The fine print (1 page)**
- Brand safety: what categories you stream, what you don't
- Schedule reliability: average stream cadence consistency
- Code attribution method (UTM, vanity URL, promo code)
- Payment terms (typical: 50% upfront, 50% on completion)
- Exclusivity window if relevant

**Page 7 — Contact + next step (optional)**

## Cold pitch template 1: Peripherals / hardware

For keyboards, mice, headsets, monitors, gaming chairs, capture cards.

```
Subject: A note on your [specific recent thing] launch

Hi [name],

[Your most-recent product] is the thing I've been watching for — specifically
the [specific feature, e.g., 8000Hz polling on the Stealth]. About 70% of
the FPS pros in the Apex scene use it, and my audience is people trying to
get there.

I stream ranked Apex four nights a week, mostly Diamond/Masters lobbies.
The viewers who show up are competitive players who care about input
latency, sensor accuracy, and the actual difference between a Hall-effect
keyboard and a tactile one. It's the most peripherals-literate audience
on Twitch that's also small enough to actually convert on a promo code.

Quick numbers:
- Average CCV peak: 800 (Tues-Fri, 8pm-12am MT)
- Average CCV across full stream: 540
- Stream cadence: 4 nights/week, 4 hours/night
- Followers: 14,200 / Active subs: 240 / Discord: 1,820 members
- Geography: 58% US, 22% Canada, 12% UK
- Engagement: ~3.2 chat messages per CCV per hour
- Past sponsor: ran a 3-stream HyperX integration in Q1, code [redacted]
  pulled 47 conversions (their attribution, shared with permission)

What I'd love to discuss:
- A 3-stream pilot with logo overlay + one ~90s natural demo per stream
- I'd write the integration; you'd review
- Flat rate in the $1,800-2,400 range for the 3-pack, depending on
  whether you'd want a Discord pinned post and a single Twitter
  cross-post included

If a 3-pack feels too soon, I can do a single-stream pilot first so you
can see how the integration lands. Happy to send the media kit and a
60-second clip from the HyperX run.

— Dex
twitch.tv/deadpan_dex

---
Things to verify before sending:
- The "8000Hz polling on the Stealth" reference — confirm this is real and
  the current spec
- The "70% of FPS pros" figure — confirm or soften the language
- HyperX 47-conversion number — confirm HyperX has given you permission
  to reference it
- $1,800-2,400 range — confirm against your current rate card
```

## The follow-up cadence

Most cold sponsor pitches don't get a reply the first time. The follow-up cadence below has worked for streamers in the 500-3,000 CCV range.

**Day 0:** Send the cold pitch.

**Day 5:** If no reply, send a single follow-up. Keep it short.

```
Subject: Re: A note on your [specific recent thing] launch

Hi [name],

Just wanted to surface this in case it got buried — I know inbound is
loud over there. Happy to send the media kit if it's a fit, or hear a
"not right now, but later" if the timing's off.

— Dex
```

**Day 14:** Single re-up, with one new piece of info — a recent stream highlight, a milestone you hit, a new audience segment you added. Don't apologize for following up.

```
Subject: One more nudge — [new info]

Hi [name],

Following up one more time — last night's stream peaked at 1,100 CCV on a
ranked Apex finals (Diamond grind to Masters), which is a new high.
Mentioning it because if your team is reconsidering the FPS audience for
[brand] in Q2, this kind of momentum is worth a conversation.

If it's not the right fit right now, all good — I'll stop the nudges. If
it is, happy to send the deck.

— Dex
```

**Day 14+:** If no reply, stop. Don't pitch the same brand again for at least 3 months. If a brand actively went silent after engaging, wait 6 months minimum.

## What NOT to put in a pitch

Things that get streamers ignored or blacklisted:

- Follower count as the lead number (sponsors care about engaged audience, not total)
- Inflated numbers — every brand checks SullyGnome or Twitch Tracker before replying
- "I've been a huge fan for years" without a specific reference (sponsors get this 50x/day)
- "Let me know!" as the close (no specific ask)
- A photo of a closet full of products from previous sponsors — reads as desperate
- "DM friendly" — keep it email
- More than one emoji, total
- Reply-all to a marketing inbox cc'ing other brands
