# Fit-for-Duty / Return-to-Work Letter Template

Use this when:
- A worker is returning from a medical leave (>3 days, or any time involving a healthcare provider)
- A supervisor has flagged a concern about a worker's ability to perform safely
- A near-miss or incident has triggered a review

This template generates a letter that's tight on legal language but reads like a human wrote it. It does NOT replace medical advice or your company's specific HR procedure.

---

Paste this prompt with the [BRACKETED] sections filled in:

---

You are helping me write a fit-for-duty letter for an employee in a construction trades context. Your output should:

- Address the employee directly, by name
- Be 200-400 words, no longer
- Use plain language — no legal jargon
- State the specific accommodations or restrictions clearly (or state "no restrictions" if applicable)
- Reference the documentation we received (doctor's note, IME, etc.) without quoting it
- End with a clear next-step (start date, who to report to, what to do if conditions change)
- Be on company letterhead style — assume the company name will be added at the top

Tone: respectful, professional, supportive but unambiguous. Do not editorialize about whether the employee should or shouldn't return; that decision is already made.

## What this letter is NOT

- A medical opinion. Do not list the diagnosis or specific medical details.
- A legal contract. If this is a serious case (modified duties under WCB, accommodation under human rights legislation), have an HR/legal review.
- A performance review.

## Example output

> **Acme Mechanical Ltd.**
> May 14, 2026
>
> Dear Jordan,
>
> Thank you for working with us during your medical leave. Based on the clearance letter we received from your physician dated May 12, 2026, and a conversation with the WCB case manager, we're confirming your return to work effective Tuesday, May 20, 2026.
>
> Your role on return will be **modified duties** for the first four weeks:
>
> - No lifting over 10 kg
> - No work above shoulder height
> - 6-hour shifts (Mon-Fri, 7am-1pm)
> - No work at heights >1.8m
>
> After four weeks, we'll check in with you and with your physician about transitioning back to full duties.
>
> On your first day back, please report to Drew at the Foothills jobsite trailer at 7:00 AM. He's been briefed on your modified duty plan and will help you settle back in.
>
> If at any point you feel the restrictions need to change — either tighter or looser — call me directly at 403-555-0142. The goal is to get you back to full duty safely, and that takes communication.
>
> We're glad to have you back.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> Chris Holwell
> Superintendent, Acme Mechanical Ltd.
