# 退款与 escalation

每个模板三种语气。挑与你品牌匹配的。**不要混合**。

---

## 退款 —— 全额同意

### 正式

> Subject: Re: Refund request —— order #38291
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> Thank you for reaching out. I've processed a full refund of $129.00 to your original payment method. You should see it return in 3-5 business days, depending on your bank.
>
> If you have any further concerns, please reply to this email and I'll be happy to assist.
>
> Best regards,
> Sarah Chen
> Customer Support

### 友好

> Subject: Re: Refund request —— order #38291
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> Refunded — $129.00 is on its way back to the card you used. Most banks post it in 3-5 business days.
>
> Anything else I can help with, just reply.
>
> Sarah

### 温暖

> Subject: Re: Refund request —— order #38291
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> Done — $129 is heading back to your card. Should land in 3-5 business days, sometimes faster.
>
> Sorry it didn't work out this time. If you ever want to give it another shot, I'll keep your account on file so you don't have to start over.
>
> Sarah

## 退款 —— 拒绝(附备选)

场景同 SKILL.md 范例 —— 47 天前买的年度订阅,窗口 30 天。

### 正式

> Subject: Re: Refund request —— order #38291
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> Thank you for your message. I've reviewed your account and your subscription was purchased on March 28, 2026, which places this request outside our 30-day refund window.
>

>
> A few options that may help:
>
> - Pausing your subscription for up to 90 days at no charge
> - Switching to monthly billing for any remaining unused time
> - A 1:1 onboarding call to ensure you're getting full value from the features you have access to
>
> Please let me know which, if any, you'd like to explore.
>
> Best regards,
> Sarah Chen
> Customer Support

## Escalation 承认

场景:客户报了一个你自己修不了的账单问题。Escalate 给账单团队,他们有 24 小时内部 SLA。

### 正式

> Subject: Re: Billing discrepancy —— account 18391
>
> Hi Priya,
>
> Thank you for flagging this. I've escalated your case to our billing team, who will review your account history and respond within 24 business hours.
>
> In the meantime, no further charges will process on your account. Please reply to this thread with any additional context you'd like reviewed.
>
> Best regards,
> Sarah Chen
> Customer Support

### 友好

>
> Hi Priya,
>

>
> If you remember anything else worth flagging before they reach out, just reply here and I'll add it to the case.
>
> Sarah

> If you believe this was a mistake, please reply with any context and I'll review further.

> Best regards,
> Sarah Chen

## 关于定制这些模板

- **把 "Sarah Chen / Sarah" 替换为实际 rep 的名字。** 不要 "Customer Support Team" 签。
- **原因行应引用你的产品/政策,不通用。** "47 days past the 30-day window" 胜过 "outside our policy"。
- **拒绝退款里的备选应是你能做的真实事情**,不是渴望式空话。暂停、降级、账户余额、免费 setup call、免费一个月、一个月免费升级 —— 挑你团队**有权**提供的。
- **在温暖语气里,"I won't pretend the line is arbitrary even when it's a few days over" 是关键动作。** 它传达你是**看到他们看到的同一条线**的人。**不要**过度使用,但**也不要**跳过。
- **Subject line 保持短而具体。** 包含工单/订单/账户号。客户会交叉对比。
