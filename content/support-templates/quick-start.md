# Quick Start — Customer Support Templates

You'll be running in under a minute.

## ChatGPT, Claude (web), or Gemini

1. Open the tool
2. Paste the contents of `optimization-pack.md` into the system prompt / custom instructions / project knowledge field
3. Start asking it to draft refund responses, escalation acknowledgements, lost-order replies, or NPS detractor outreach — in formal, friendly, or warm tone

## Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex (SKILL.md path)

1. Open Terminal (or your code editor)
2. Drop the kit folder into `~/.claude/skills/support-templates/` (Claude Code) or paste `SKILL.md` at your project root (Cursor / Codex)
3. Type what you want — Claude picks up the skill automatically

## Test it works

Paste this: "Write a denied refund response in warm tone. Customer bought an annual plan 47 days ago, our window is 30 days, they want a full refund. Offer 3 alternatives."

If you get back a reply that:
- Says no in the first paragraph
- Doesn't quote a policy clause
- Offers three concrete alternatives (pause, monthly downgrade, setup call, account credit — that kind of thing)
- Ends with one ask and a real name
- Doesn't contain the words "sincerely apologize for any inconvenience"

— the kit is loaded right.
