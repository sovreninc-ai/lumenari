# 邮件序列

每家 Shopify 店都该跑的五个序列。弃购、欢迎、浏览弃购、购后、win-back。每个针对 Klaviyo(或 Mailchimp)配置 —— 结构与平台无关。

每封邮件含:subject line(50 字符以内)、preview text(90 字符以内)、body。工具包可以**一次提示出全套**,或**一次出一封**,视用户需要。

---

## 弃购序列(3 封)

DTC 最高杠杆 flow。行业标准回收率是弃购的 10-15%;调优良好的 flow 可达 20%+。

**发送时间:** 弃购后 1 小时、24 小时、72 小时。

### 提示词

```
你在写 3 封信弃购序列。

品牌:[3-5 条语气注]
平均 AOV:[$X —— 影响 email 3 是否含折扣]
产品类目:[apparel / home / beauty / food / supplements / 等]
常见异议:[列出 2-3 个客户犹豫点 —— 运费、尺寸、退货政策、成分问题]

输出 3 封:
- Email 1(弃购后 1 小时):友好提醒,无折扣,聚焦他们留下的产品
- Email 2(24 小时后):回应**最可能**的异议
- Email 3(72 小时后):激励 —— 折扣、免运,或社会证明,视 AOV

每封:subject line(50 字符以内)、preview text(90 字符以内)、body(120-200 字)。
不写禁用词。不假紧迫。
```

### 范例 —— 美妆品牌,AOV $60

**Email 1(1 小时后):**
```
Subject(32 字符): Did your cart wander off?
Preview(78 字符): The ceramide moisturizer you were looking at is still here when you're ready.

Hi [first name],

Looks like you were checking out the ceramide moisturizer earlier. It's still in your cart if you want to pick up where you left off.

A few things people usually want to know before ordering:
- Free shipping over $50
- 60-day return policy, even on opened bottles
- Ships within 1-2 business days from Toronto

[Return to your cart →]

If you decided it wasn't the right fit, no worries. We're easy to reach if you want to ask anything.

— [Brand]
```

**Email 2(24 小时后 —— 回应 "这是否适合我的肌肤" 异议):**
```
Subject(44 字符): About the moisturizer in your cart
Preview(82 字符): Quick note on what makes ours work for sensitive, over-stripped skin types.

Hi [first name],

Quick note — the most common question we get on the ceramide moisturizer is "will this work for me?"

It's built for:
- Skin that's reactive to actives or fragrance
- People using retinol or other actives who need a low-irritation base

It's probably not the right fit if:
- You're looking for a heavy occlusive overnight cream

If that lines up, [the bottle in your cart] is here.

If you want to ask anything before ordering, reply to this email. I read these.

— [Founder name]
```

**Email 3(72 小时后 —— 软激励):**
```
Subject(37 字符): One last note before we let it go
Preview(76 字符): 10% off on the cart you started, plus a quick reminder of why people stick.

Going to leave this one in your hands. The cart you started a few days ago — we'll hold it for the next 48 hours with 10% off if you want it.

Use code STILLHERE at checkout.

For what it's worth: we have a 4.8-star rating across 1,400+ reviews, and the moisturizer is our top-reordered product (most customers come back for a second bottle within 10 weeks).

[Use STILLHERE on your cart →]

Either way, hope you find what you're looking for.

— [Brand]
```

## 欢迎序列(30 天 4 封)

发给首次邮件订阅者(通过 pop-up、footer 表单或购后注册)。30 天 4 封。

**发送时间:** 立即、第 3 天、第 10 天、第 28 天。

### 提示词

```
你在为新订阅写 4 封欢迎序列。

品牌:[3-5 条语气注]
品牌故事(1-3 句):[粘 —— 这个品牌为什么存在]
产品系列:[3-5 个类目或主打]
注册激励:[我们承诺的 —— 10% off、免运等]

输出 4 封:
- Email 1(立即):致谢 + 首购激励码
- Email 2(第 3 天):品牌故事 —— 最长 1 分钟阅读
- Email 3(第 10 天):畅销或如何选择适合你的
- Email 4(第 28 天):社区 / 求评 / 推荐 CTA

每封:subject line(50 字符以内)、preview text(90 字符以内)、body(150-250 字)。
不写禁用词。
```

### 范例 —— 家居品牌,注册激励:免运

**Email 1(立即):**
```
Subject(28 字符): Welcome — your code's below
Preview(75 字符): Free shipping on your first order, plus a quick rundown of what we make.

Hi [first name],

Welcome. Use code FIRSTSHIP at checkout for free shipping on your first order.

We make small-batch home goods — candles, ceramics, textiles — out of a shop in Vancouver. Everything is made in batches of 20-40, and most of it sells out within a month of restock.

A few things worth knowing:
- New batches drop the first Friday of every month
- We email subscribers 24 hours before public release
- Returns are accepted on unopened candles and unused textiles within 30 days

If you have questions before you order, reply to this email. It comes to my desk.

— [Founder name]

[Code: FIRSTSHIP — Free shipping, first order]
```

**Email 2(第 3 天 —— 品牌故事):**
```
Subject(33 字符): How this place came to exist
Preview(85 字符): A short story about why we make beeswax candles and slow-fire ceramics in 2026.

[150-200 字品牌故事 —— 保持脚踏实地;不写 "passion" 话术;
展示品牌存在的真实理由]
```

**Email 3(第 10 天 —— 畅销 / 指引):**
```
Subject(42 字符): If you're not sure where to start...
Preview(88 字符): A few of our best-reordered things, plus what most people pair them with.

[150-250 字产品指南;3-4 个畅销,每个一行原因]
```

**Email 4(第 28 天 —— 推荐 / 求评 CTA):**
```
Subject(37 字符): A small ask if you ordered
Preview(89 字符): If something you bought landed well — would you tell us about it?

[对他们买过的简短求评,或若未买过则推荐 CTA]
```

## 浏览弃购(2 封)

发给浏览过产品但未加车的订阅。意图比弃购低 —— 语气更软。

**发送时间:** 产品浏览后 4 小时、48 小时。

### 紧凑提示词

```
品牌:[3-5 条语气注]
浏览过的产品:[名称 + 一句描述]
有人浏览但不加车的常见原因:[粘 —— 价格、尺寸、成分、没时间]

输出:
- Email 1(浏览后 4 小时):"只是把链接给你" 的轻消息
- Email 2(浏览后 48 小时):一条有用信息(评论节选、对常见异议的一行回答)

每封:subject 50 字符以内、preview 90 字符以内、body 80-150 字。
不写禁用词。本序列中**无折扣**。
```

## 购后序列(3 封)

订单完成后发。订单确认、发货通知、求评。

**发送时间:** 立即、发货确认时、送达后 10-14 天(消耗品)或 21-30 天(需要考虑的购买)。

### 模式

前两封(订单确认、发货)主要是事务性,但工具包让它们比默认 Shopify 模板**更温暖**。第三封(求评)才是写作真正重要的地方。

```
品牌:[语气注]
刚送达的产品:[名称]
评论平台:[Judge.me / Yotpo / Loox / Shopify 原生]
激励(若有):[下次购买折扣、抽奖、无]

输出求评邮件:
- Subject(50 字符以内)
- Preview(90 字符以内)
- Body(100-180 字)
- **一**个清晰留评 CTA
- 承认评论是个小请求,也是真实帮助
- 不写禁用词
- **不**承诺评论会被发布或应当是 5 星
```

### 范例 —— 补剂品牌

```
Subject(39 字符): Hope the magnesium is working out
Preview(88 字符): Two weeks in is when most people notice — quick ask if you have a sec.

Hi [first name],

Two weeks in is usually when people start noticing whether the magnesium is doing what they hoped, or whether it's not the right fit.

If you've got 60 seconds, would you leave a quick review? Honest is more useful to other people than positive — if it didn't work for you, we'd rather know.

[Leave a review →]

And if anything came up — the wrong product, capsule trouble, anything — reply to this email and we'll sort it.

Thanks for trying us out.

— [Founder]
```

## Win-back(2 封)

发给 60-120 天未下单的客户(视类目 —— 消耗品更短,考虑型更长)。

**发送时间:** 沉睡 60 天、沉睡 90 天。

### 提示词

```
品牌:[语气注]
该品牌平均订单频次:[消耗品每 X 周等]
最有可能复购的产品:[列出 2-3]

输出 2 封:
- Email 1(60 天):无压 check-in,问一切是否好
- Email 2(90 天):低摩擦激励 —— 15% off、免运,或补货提醒

每封:subject 50 字符以内、preview 90 字符以内、body 100-180 字。
不写禁用词。**不**暗示客户落后了。**尊重自主权**。
```

### 范例 —— 咖啡品牌

**Email 1(60 天):**
```
Subject(38 字符): No pressure — quick check-in
Preview(75 字符): Just making sure your coffee situation hasn't gone sideways since spring.

Hi [first name],

It's been about two months since your last order — wanted to check in. No pressure to reorder; just making sure we didn't drop the ball somewhere.

If you ran out and got busy, [the Ethiopia Guji you bought last time is still on the menu]. If you're trying something new from a different roaster, that's great — let me know what you ended up liking and I might add it to a sourcing list.

— [Founder]
```

## 好邮件序列不做什么

- **Email 1 就给折扣。** 教会客户等折扣,你就教会他们**永远不付全价**。
- **不分类目用同样通用文案。** 补剂的弃购应与蜡烛的弃购**听起来不同**。
- **用假倒计时。** 客户能看穿,信任侵蚀。
- **把退订链接埋起来。** 让它**好找**。干净退订带来的 list 质量提升,**值得**那点 list 数损失。
- **像客户欠你似的发 win-back。** **尊重自主**。客户有**不**回来的权利。
