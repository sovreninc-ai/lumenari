# Reference Workflows — Office Manager Pack

Worked examples and reusable structures for the things you write five times a year. Copy, adapt, send.

## 1. Vendor quote request — full template + worked example

**Template:**

```
Subject: Quick quote request — [thing] for [Company]

Hi [Name],

I'm [Name], office manager at [Company]. We're a [headcount]-person [industry] team in [city, neighborhood]. I'm pricing out [thing] and would love a quote from you.

Scope as I understand it today:
- [detail 1]
- [detail 2]
- [detail 3]
- [detail 4]

Ideal timing: [window]
Budget zone: [range, OR "flexible if scope fits"]

Could you send back:
1. A written quote
2. A sample SOW or agreement
3. Your typical lead time from contract sign to start

Happy to jump on a 15-min call if it's easier — I'm at [phone] or this email works.

Thanks,
[First name]
[Title, Company]
```

**Worked example — corporate catering for a lunch-and-learn:**

```
Subject: Quick quote request — catered lunch for 45 — Sproutfield

Hi Daniella,

I'm Jamie, office manager at Sproutfield. We're a 65-person product company in Toronto's Liberty Village. I'm pricing out a catered lunch for a Wednesday lunch-and-learn series and would love a quote from Olive & Oak.

Scope:
- 45 people, Wednesdays, 12:00 drop-off
- 8-week pilot, optionally extending if it works
- Lunch boxes preferred over buffet (faster turnover, less waste)
- 4 dietary buckets: standard, vegetarian, gluten-free, halal
- We want labeled allergens visible on each box

Ideal timing: first lunch on April 16.
Budget zone: $18-22/head, flexible if scope fits.

Could you send back:
1. A written quote per head
2. A sample weekly menu rotation
3. Your typical lead time for menu lock and delivery confirmation

Happy to jump on a 15-min call — I'm at 416-555-0188 or this email.

Thanks,
Jamie
Office Manager, Sproutfield
```

## 2. Internal announcement — building closure / facilities

**Worked example — HVAC weekend work:**

```
Subject: Building closed Sat 8 AM - 2 PM — HVAC work

Team — heads-up: the landlord is replacing a rooftop HVAC unit this Saturday. Building is closed 8 AM - 2 PM, no badge access.

If you were planning to come in for weekend catch-up, route to home or a coffee shop. The unit being swapped is the one that serves the south side (your usual Monday cool-air complaints, fixed).

Power and internet stay up — only physical access is restricted.

Next update Monday morning if anything's still off when we open.

Thanks,
Jamie
```

**Worked example — kitchen behavior change:**

```
Subject: A small kitchen ask

Quick one: the dishwasher's been running 3+ times a day because mugs and dishes pile up by lunch. If you can rinse and load whatever you used before heading back to your desk, we cut that to one run and the kitchen stops smelling like old coffee by 4 PM.

That's it. Thanks for humoring me.

— Jamie
```

## 3. Small-event planning doc — worked example

**Event:** Half-day team offsite, 45 people, Calgary, March, $120/person budget

```
EVENT: Q1 Team Day
Date: Tuesday, March 19, 1 PM - 5 PM
Headcount: 45 confirmed
Budget: $5,400 total = $120/head
Goal: connection after a heads-down quarter. Founder wants something more substantive than a games night. No alcohol — it's a Tuesday afternoon.

VENUE OPTIONS

1. Studio Bell — National Music Centre, East Village
   Capacity: 60 (private room). Cost: $1,800 flat + $40/head catered. AV: yes. Catering: in-house only.
   Trade-off: gorgeous space, the catering is mandatory and limits us on dietary flexibility.

2. The Commons — co-working space, Mission
   Capacity: 80. Cost: $900 flat. AV: yes. Catering: BYO.
   Trade-off: less character than Studio Bell, more flexibility on catering and agenda, parking is street-only.

3. The Royal Theatre — private cinema, Downtown
   Capacity: 100. Cost: $2,200 flat. AV: theatre-grade. Catering: external.
   Trade-off: incredible AV for a speaker segment, weak for a workshop format. Best if we lean talk-heavy.

Recommended: The Commons. Best balance of cost, flexibility, and budget left over for catering.

CATERING OPTIONS

1. Pho Bistro (Vietnamese, drop-off boxes)
   $22/head, dietary buckets covered (standard / vegetarian / gluten-free / halal), 24-hr menu lock.

2. Sidewalk Citizen (Mediterranean, family-style platters)
   $28/head, same dietary buckets, 48-hr lock. Better for sit-down formats.

Recommended: Sidewalk Citizen for sit-down dining + 45-min workshop format.

AGENDA

12:45 - 1:00   Arrival, coffee/tea, name tags at door
1:00 - 1:15    Founder kickoff: what Q1 taught us (10 min + 5 Q&A buffer)
1:15 - 2:30    Workshop: "What we should keep / stop / start doing as a team" — small groups of 6, two rounds
2:30 - 2:45    Break, snacks out
2:45 - 3:30    Cross-team panel: 3 people share what they wish other teams knew about their work
3:30 - 4:30    Family-style dinner + open table conversation
4:30 - 5:00    Founder closing + thank-you + wrap

LOGISTICS

- RSVP deadline: March 8 (11 days before)
- Dietary form: linked in the RSVP, defaults vegetarian if unmarked
- Transit / parking: 5-min walk from C-Train Erlton station; street parking $2/hr 2-hr limit
- AV setup: arrive 45 min early, mic check, slides on USB AND in Drive as backup
- Photos: Ben from marketing has volunteered, 30 candid shots through the afternoon
- Day-of point person: Jamie (me), reachable on Slack DM or 403-555-0188
- Rain plan: indoor venue, n/a

COMMS

- Save the date: this Friday in #general + email
- Full invite + RSVP link: March 4
- Reminder + dietary form nudge: March 11
- Final logistics (address, transit, what to bring): March 18 morning

POST-EVENT

- Thank-you note to The Commons + Sidewalk Citizen, copy founder
- 30 photos to #culture channel within 48 hrs
- Optional 3-question feedback survey: "what was best / what felt off / what should we try next time"
```

## 4. Facilities ticket — worked examples

**To the landlord:**

```
Subject: Freight elevator out of service — Suite 401 — since Tuesday morning

Hi Marcel,

Reporting that the freight elevator on the east side of the building has been out of service since Tuesday morning around 10 AM. We've got a furniture delivery scheduled Thursday at 9 AM that needs it — currently no plan B for the dock.

What I've already tried: confirmed with reception and with two of our team that the call button is non-responsive on floors 1, 3, and 4. Passenger elevator is fine.

Impact: blocking Thursday's delivery if it's not back by then.
Best window for the engineer: any time today before 5 PM, or tomorrow before noon.
Reachable: 403-555-0188 or email.

Thanks,
Jamie
```

**To the IT MSP:**

```
Subject: Conference room AV — Room 3B — black screen

Hi Helpdesk,

Room 3B's main TV is showing a black screen on HDMI input as of this morning's 9 AM standup. The room itself is fine — projector input works, just not the main TV.

What I've tried: power cycled the TV (twice), swapped HDMI cables (used the spare from Room 2A), tried two different laptops, checked the input source. Same result.

Impact: room is unusable for any video call until fixed. Has a 1 PM and 3 PM booking today that I can reroute to Room 2A.
Best window: before 1 PM if at all possible, or any time tomorrow morning.
Reachable: in the office today, 403-555-0188.

Thanks,
Jamie
```

## 7. The 20-minute par-level build

If you've never done a par-level list, block 20 minutes. Walk the kitchen, the supply closet, the printer area, and the tech storage. For each item that you've run out of in the last 6 months: write it down, write where you buy it, write how many you ideally keep on hand, write how long it takes to arrive, write what triggers reorder. Type it up. Save it shared with whoever covers when you're out. You just built the system your future self has been wanting for two years.
