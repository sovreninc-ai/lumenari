## 1. Hourly local move quote — 3-bedroom

```
Subject: Quote — Local move, [origin] to [destination], [date]

Hi [customer first name],

Here's the quote based on what you described. Valid 30 days.

PROPERTY
3-bedroom house (origin) to 4-bedroom house (destination), approx 12 km across town. Ground floor access at both ends, normal carry distance (under 50 ft each side), no elevators or stairs to worry about.

SERVICE TYPE
Hourly (non-binding). We bill from when we leave the yard to when we get back. Travel time both ways is included.

CREW + TRUCK
1 truck (26-foot), 3 movers. A 3-bedroom in your area typically runs this configuration.

TIME ESTIMATE
5-7 hours including 30 minutes drive each way. Most 3-bedrooms in this distance bracket land in that window. Could go faster if you're well-boxed; could go longer if there are specialty items or boxes that didn't make the inventory.

HOURLY RATE
[QUOTE: per-hour rate including truck + 3 movers]
Estimated total range: [QUOTE: low total] to [QUOTE: high total]

We'll text you at the 3-4 hour mark with a pace update so you're not surprised at the end.

ADDITIONAL CHARGES IF APPLICABLE
- Long carry (over 50 ft from truck to entry): [QUOTE: rate]
- Stair carry (per flight above ground floor): [QUOTE: rate]
- Shuttle service (if a 26-ft truck can't access): [QUOTE: rate]
- Specialty items (piano, safe, hot tub, pool table): quoted separately — please let us know in advance
- Fuel surcharge: [QUOTE: rate or "included"]

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Furniture pads (blankets), shrink wrap for upholstered items
- Dollies, hand trucks
- Standard floor protection
- Drive time both ways
- Loading and unloading labour

WHAT'S NOT INCLUDED
- Boxes (you can buy from us at cost or provide your own)
- Packing service (available as a separate add-on at [QUOTE: rate])
- Specialty item handling beyond standard furniture
- Disconnection/reconnection of appliances (we move them, but plumbing/electrical hookups are on you or a contractor)

INSURANCE
We carry $2M CGL coverage — certificate available on request.
Released-value protection is included at the legal minimum of $[0.60/lb in US, similar in CA] per pound per article. Most customers want more than that. Full-value protection (replacement coverage) is available at [QUOTE: % of declared value]. We can talk through which makes sense for your situation.

TERMS
Payment due day-of completion. We take credit card, e-transfer, or certified cheque.
24 hours notice on cancellation or 50% no-show fee.
We confirm by phone the day before with arrival window (2-hour) and your lead mover's first name.

ON THE DAY
- Day before, evening: confirmation call/text
- Morning of: text when crew leaves the yard
- Midpoint: pace update + ETA at destination
- End: final walkthrough with you, signature on bill of lading, photo of empty truck

Reply to confirm and we'll lock in your date.

Thanks,
[Owner first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Per-hour rate
- Estimated low/high totals
- Long carry, stair carry, shuttle rates
- Fuel surcharge approach (built in vs. separate line)
- Released-value protection language — confirm exact $/lb for your jurisdiction
- Full-value protection % rate
```

## 2. Binding quote — completed inventory walkthrough

```
Subject: Binding quote — [origin] to [destination], [date]

Hi [customer first name],

Here's your binding quote based on the in-home walkthrough I did on [date]. Valid 30 days.

SERVICE TYPE
Binding (flat-rate). The total below is the price regardless of how long the move takes — unless scope changes (you add items, or access at either end is different than what I walked).

INVENTORY (from walkthrough)

Master bedroom:
- King bed + frame
- 6-drawer dresser + mirror
- 2 nightstands
- 8 linear ft of hanging clothes (approx 8 wardrobe boxes worth)
- 4 medium boxes (linens, accessories)

Bedroom 2:
- Queen bed + frame
- 4-drawer dresser
- 1 nightstand
- 5 linear ft hanging
- 3 medium boxes

Bedroom 3:
- Twin bed + frame
- Small dresser
- 1 bookshelf (5-shelf, you'll empty)
- 4 boxes

Office:
- L-shaped desk
- Office chair
- 2 filing cabinets (will be empty)
- Bookshelf (you'll empty)
- Computer setup (you'll pack)

Living room:
- 3-seat sofa
- 1 armchair
- Coffee table + 2 end tables
- TV stand
- 65" TV (mounted — we'll unmount and box; bring the mount or leave it)
- 2 bookshelves (you'll empty)

Kitchen:
- 12 dish-pack boxes (estimated)
- Small appliances (toaster, coffee maker, mixer)
- Kitchen table + 6 chairs
- Bar cart

Bathrooms:
- 3 small boxes (toiletries, towels)

Garage:
- Tool chest (rolling — drawers will be emptied)
- 2 bikes
- Lawn equipment (gas drained per discussion)
- 6 stackable bins of holiday/storage

Specialty items:
- Upright piano (confirmed acoustic, full size) — we'll handle with our piano team
- 1 large framed painting (4x6 ft) — custom crate, [QUOTE: crate rate]

Access:
- Origin: ground floor, normal driveway access, no elevator. Lead mover will pull up to the side door.
- Destination: 2nd floor walk-up over 1 flight (15 stairs). Confirmed truck can park in front (you'll reserve with cones the morning of).

CREW + TRUCK
2 trucks (26-ft each), 5 movers total. Piano team arrives separately at 10 AM.

BINDING TOTAL
[QUOTE: binding total all-in]

This includes:
- Both trucks, full crew, drive time
- All standard pads, dollies, shrink wrap
- Piano handling
- Custom crate for the large painting
- Stair carry at destination (15 stairs, 1 flight)
- Fuel and tolls
- Standard $2M CGL coverage and released-value protection

NOT INCLUDED
- Boxes you'll need to pack — you said you have these covered
- Full-value protection (released-value is the included minimum) — available at [QUOTE: % of declared value]
- Disconnection/reconnection of appliances or fixtures

SCOPE CHANGE POLICY
If items are added on move day that weren't on this inventory, we'll re-quote on the spot before loading. You can decide to add at the new price or leave items behind. We don't load surprise items without re-quoting.

DEPOSIT + PAYMENT
- 30% deposit on contract signing — [QUOTE: deposit amount]
- Balance due day-of completion
- Payment: credit card, e-transfer, or certified cheque

ON THE DAY
Standard comms: day-before confirmation, day-of start text, midpoint update, final walkthrough.

Reply to confirm and we'll lock the date with the deposit.

Thanks,
[Owner first name]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Binding total
- Crate rate for the painting
- Deposit amount
- Full-value protection rate
- Confirm piano team availability for the date
- Confirm parking arrangement at destination (cones / city permit?)
```

## 3. Late-arrival script (the most-used in the business)

```
DAY-OF, RUNNING 90 MINUTES BEHIND ON PREVIOUS JOB:

Hi [customer first name], it's [dispatcher / owner first name] from [company].

Crew is running about 90 minutes behind from their previous job — a customer there had a couple of items not on the inventory and we re-quoted and loaded them. New arrival window for you is [updated time, 2-hour window].

Lead mover [first name] will text you when they're 15 minutes out so you know exactly when to expect them. Sorry for the shift — wanted you to know now rather than at the door.

If this doesn't work for you, call me back at [owner phone] and we'll figure out a plan.

---

DAY-OF, ON A NORMAL-PACE JOB AT THE 4-HOUR MARK OF AN ESTIMATED 6-HOUR MOVE:

Hi [customer first name], quick update from [lead mover first name] —

We're about halfway done. Truck is loaded, leaving for [destination] by [time]. Looking like a 6-7 hour total move based on pace, which is in your original estimate.

See you there in about [X minutes].

---

DAY-OF, CREEPING PAST THE ORIGINAL ESTIMATE:

Hi [customer first name], heads-up from [lead mover first name] —

We're about [X] hours in and we've got about [Y] more to go. Looking like 8-9 hours total, which is over the original 6-7 estimate. A couple of factors: [the boxes turned out to be heavier than expected / the long carry at destination is adding time / specify what's actually happening — don't be vague].

Wanted to flag it now rather than at the end. Total at the current pace will be around [QUOTE: revised total] based on the hourly rate.

Let me know if you have questions.

---
Nothing flagged — adapt the bracketed pieces to your specifics.
```

## 5. Claims response — scratched dresser

```
Subject: Re: Damage from move on [date]

Hi [customer first name],

Thanks for letting me know about the dresser. Sorry to hear it.

I want to look into this carefully before I respond, so a couple of questions:

1. Can you send 2-3 photos of the damage? Close-up plus one wider shot showing the dresser in your room.
2. Roughly when did you notice it — at unload, the next day, or later?
3. Approximately what's the replacement value or repair estimate you'd be looking at?

While you're sending those, I'm going to pull the bill of lading from your move and our inventory walkthrough notes to check whether this dresser was flagged as having pre-existing condition issues, and what level of valuation coverage you elected.

I'll have a response back to you within [X business days] of getting your photos and notes. Most claims like this we resolve cleanly — either through a payment if it's clearly on us, or through a repair via our furniture contractor.

Thanks for being direct about it rather than just leaving it. Either way I want to make this right within what's fair.

[Owner first name]
[Owner phone]

---
Numbers and assumptions you should plug in:
- Confirm your standard claim resolution window (14 business days is common)
- Confirm whether you have a furniture repair contractor on call
- For claims that may exceed [your threshold], add legal review step before the final response
```
