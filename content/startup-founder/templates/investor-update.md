# Monthly Investor Update — the 5 questions

A monthly update has one job: keep investors warm enough that when you need them, they answer the email.

Five questions, in order, that every update should answer:

1. **What did you ship this month?** (concrete, not "made progress")
2. **What are the numbers?** (revenue, burn, runway, key product metrics)
3. **What's the next 30 days?** (what you'll have shipped/closed by next update)
4. **What's the ask?** (a specific intro, a hire, a piece of advice, or "nothing this month")
5. **What did you learn?** (one customer insight, one team insight, one personal insight — pick one, not all three)

Length: 400-700 words. Anything longer and they skim. Anything shorter and you've underdelivered.

---

## The system prompt

You are writing a monthly investor update on behalf of a founder. Your defaults:

- Founder's voice, first person.
- Direct and specific. "Closed three new clubs" not "saw growth in customer base."
- One paragraph per section. No bullet lists for filler — only when the content is genuinely a list.
- Numbers in monospace or bold so they pop on a phone screen.
- Subject line is the most important line. Use it.
- Always end with the ask. ALWAYS. Even if the ask is "no ask this month."

You DO NOT:
- Hide bad news
- Use the phrase "exciting opportunities"
- Open with "I hope this finds you well"
- Mention the weather, the season, or "what a year it's been"

## Example output

> **Subject:** Lumenari · May update · 47 → 89 paid clubs, $5.6k MRR
>
> Hi all,
>
> May was a build month that turned into a sales month. We shipped the parent-app v1 onboarding flow on the 4th and the field-resource module on the 19th. Both went live to all 47 existing clubs without a single P0 ticket.
>
> **The numbers:**
> - MRR: **$5,620** (up from $2,840 in April)
> - New clubs: **42** (89 paid, total)
> - Burn: **$1,400/month** (mostly Supabase + Vercel + Resend)
> - Runway: **18 months** at current burn, before any new revenue
> - Retention: 96% MoM (one churn — they moved to a different sport we don't support yet)
>
> **Next 30 days:** Locking down the tryouts module so we can land the two hockey clubs that have been waiting. Goal is **150 paid clubs by July 1** and **$10k MRR**.
>
> **The ask:** I'm hiring my first full-time engineer (Vancouver/Calgary, remote). If you know a strong TypeScript + Supabase generalist who's done multi-tenant work, please intro. The JD is at lumenari.io/jobs/eng-1.
>
> **What I learned:** The thing parents pay for isn't the feature list — it's the absence of TeamSnap's six-tap RSVP flow. The single biggest lever this month was cutting RSVP to two taps. That alone explains the retention number.
>
> Thanks for the support,
>
> Chris
