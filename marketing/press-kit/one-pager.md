## The problem

Generic AI prompts produce generic output. A real estate agent, a TypeScript engineer, and a personal injury lawyer all get the same vanilla "helpful assistant" by default — and end up rewriting the same setup prompt every morning. Working pros need role-specific structure, not another tip thread on Twitter.

## The solution

Lumenari is a curated catalog of 100 AI skill kits across 17 categories. Every kit ships in **4 formats** — Anthropic's SKILL.md (Claude / Cursor), Custom GPT instructions (ChatGPT), an optimization-pack.md (any chat AI), and a memory.md domain context file — plus a quick-start guide. A free recommendation wizard matches you to kits in under 60 seconds. No lock-in to one AI vendor. Ever.

## Why now

Anthropic shipped SKILL.md as a standard. OpenAI, Google, and Cursor are all converging on similar structured-skill formats. Lumenari is the catalog layer for that shift — and the bet is that **portability** wins. The pros who pay for tools don't want to rewrite their workflow every time a new model ships. They want one kit that works everywhere.
