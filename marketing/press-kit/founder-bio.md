## 200 words — full features, longer interviews

*Use when:* a journalist is writing a profile, a longform feature, or a deep-dive interview piece.

> Chris Holwell is the founder of Sovren Inc., the parent company behind a portfolio of solo-built SaaS products. His current focus is **Lumenari** (lumenari.io), a curated catalog of 100 AI skill kits across 17 categories — every kit shipped in four formats so it's portable across Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, and Gemini, with no lock-in to any single AI vendor. Lumenari is built on Anthropic's SKILL.md standard, a format Chris has been working in daily for roughly a year.
>
> Alongside Lumenari, Chris is building **Sovren Sports' Villains Hub**, a multi-sport youth platform anchored by Calgary Villains and its 3,000 players — a product whose origin he traces directly to watching his two sons play competitive soccer every weekend. He's also building **TradePass.app**, an HR SaaS for the construction industry, and is the author of an upcoming AI-illustrated kids' book, *Birth of An Idea* (ages 9-14).
>
> Chris is a journeyman pipefitter based in Calgary, Alberta, building all of it on the side while transitioning out of trades work into recurring-revenue businesses he owns. He writes about the economics of solo-founder SaaS — building digital products in 15-minute phone batches between rotating shifts.

*(200 words)*
