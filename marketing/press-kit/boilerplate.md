## 1 page (300-400 words)

*Use when:* full media kits, longform press features, investor or partner overviews, anywhere a journalist needs the complete "About Lumenari" in one block.

> **Lumenari** ([lumenari.io](https://lumenari.io)) is a curated catalog of 100 AI skill kits across 17 categories — built for working professionals who use AI every day and need it to produce role-specific output, not generic answers.
>
> The problem Lumenari solves is simple: a real estate agent, a TypeScript engineer, and a personal injury lawyer all get the same vanilla "helpful assistant" out of the box from Claude, ChatGPT, or any other major AI. The pros who actually use these tools end up rewriting the same setup prompt every morning, or duct-taping their own structured workflows together. Lumenari ships those workflows as polished, ready-to-load kits.
>
> Every kit ships in **four formats**: a SKILL.md file (Anthropic's standard, also loadable in Cursor), a Custom GPT instructions file (ChatGPT), an optimization-pack.md (works in any chat AI), and a memory.md domain-context file — plus a quick-start guide. The same kit works in Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, and Gemini. **No lock-in to any single AI vendor.**
>
> A free recommendation wizard matches users to the right kit in under 60 seconds. Individual kits start at **$14 CAD** (~$10 USD) for the 80 non-developer kits and **$19 CAD** (~$14 USD) for the 20 developer-heavy kits. Bundles run from **$35 to $249 CAD**. A Pro+ subscription is available monthly, annually, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. A public REST API exposes the catalog to developers and platforms, with tiers from a free 100-calls-per-month plan up through Pro ($99/mo), Business ($499/mo), and Enterprise.
>
> The bet behind Lumenari is **portability**. Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google are all converging on structured skill formats. The catalog that works across all of them is the durable layer.
>
> Lumenari is a **Sovren Inc.** product, founded by **Chris Holwell** — a journeyman pipefitter in Calgary, Alberta, building three SaaS businesses on the side while transitioning out of trades work. Sovren Inc. also builds Sovren Sports' Villains Hub (a multi-sport youth platform anchored by Calgary Villains and its 3,000 players) and TradePass.app (HR SaaS for the construction industry).
>

>

## Variant to use when

| Context | Variant |
|---|---|
| Press release boilerplate footer | 1 sentence |
| "About" line in a partner's announcement | 1 sentence |
| Conference program / sponsor blurb | 1 paragraph |
| Bottom of an outbound pitch email | 1 paragraph |
| Partner blog co-announcement | 1 paragraph |
| Full media kit cover page | 1 page |
| Longform press feature "About the company" | 1 page |
| Investor / partner overview deck | 1 page |
| Journalist asks "tell me about Lumenari in writing" | 1 page |

**Rule of thumb:** if the reader will see the boilerplate *next to* the actual story, use the shortest version that fits. If the boilerplate **is** the story (media kit, partner overview), use the 1-page.
