# Lumenari — Hashtag Strategy

Per-platform tactics for Twitter/X and LinkedIn. The short version: LinkedIn rewards hashtags, Twitter doesn't, and proprietary tags are a long bet that should be used sparingly until they catch on.

---

## Twitter / X

**The honest baseline:** Twitter/X is historically anti-hashtag for engagement. Posts with 3+ hashtags consistently underperform plain-text posts in the algorithm. The platform has been moving toward signal-based ranking (replies, reposts, dwell time, follow conversion) and away from tag-based discovery for at least 5 years.

**The actual levers on Twitter that beat hashtags:**

- **Reply-guy presence** — replying to bigger accounts in your niche is the single biggest growth lever, by a wide margin
- **Mentions** — @-mentioning a relevant person or brand pulls them into the conversation and surfaces the post to their followers
- **Threading** — long-form value as a thread outperforms single-tweet hashtag-stuffing
- **Quote-tweet engagement** — adding genuine commentary to relevant posts puts you in front of that author's network
- **Time-of-day** — posting when your audience is awake matters more than tags

**When hashtags DO help on Twitter (rare):**

- Discovery in known communities (#buildinpublic, #indiehackers) where users actually browse the tag feed
- Event-based discovery (live conference hashtags, launch days)
- Niche technical tags where the audience is small enough to monitor the tag (#TypeScript, #Supabase)

**Cadence rule for Lumenari on Twitter:** 0-1 hashtag per post. Never 2+. Use mentions instead 90% of the time.

### 8-10 specific Twitter hashtags Lumenari can occasionally use

| Hashtag | When to use | How often |
|---|---|---|
| `#buildinpublic` | Founder updates, milestone posts, "what I shipped" tweets | 1x per week max — this community actually browses the tag |
| `#indiehackers` | Bootstrapped solo-founder posts, revenue milestones | 1-2x per month — the IH crowd is your direct audience |
| `#AI` | Almost never — too broad to be useful, gets buried | 0-1x per month, only on big launch posts |
| `#ChatGPT` | Direct ChatGPT-related kit announcements or comparisons | Rare — once every 2-3 weeks at most |
| `#Claude` | Direct Claude-related kit announcements, SKILL.md posts | 1-2x per month — smaller community, higher signal |
| `#Cursor` | Dev kit announcements where Cursor is the target | 1-2x per month for dev posts |
| `#PromptEngineering` | Hot takes on prompt vs. structure | Rare — once a month, only on contrarian posts |
| `#SaaS` | Pricing posts, founder math posts, B2B-flavored content | 1x per week max |
| `#solofounder` | Personal-journey posts, time-management content | 1x per month max |
| `#bootstrapped` | Revenue milestones, anti-VC posts, runway content | 1x per month max |

**Mention strategy beats hashtag strategy on Twitter.** Build a list of 30-50 accounts in your space (AI tooling founders, indie devs, tech writers, vertical SMEs in your kit categories) and reply to 3-5 of them per day with genuine value. That's worth more than every hashtag combined.

## LinkedIn

**The honest baseline:** LinkedIn is pro-hashtag. The algorithm uses hashtag tags as a primary discovery signal — they appear in the "topics you follow" feed and in topic-based browse surfaces. 3-5 hashtags per post is the sweet spot. More than 5 starts to look spammy and degrades reach.

**Hashtag rules for LinkedIn:**

- 3-5 hashtags per post, placed at the END of the post (not inline)
- Mix of broad (high-volume, lower competition rank) and niche (lower-volume, higher-rank potential)
- Be consistent — using the same 5-7 hashtags repeatedly helps the algorithm categorize your account
- Match the hashtags to the post's actual content — generic hashtag-stuffing hurts more than it helps

## Cross-platform comparison table

| Dimension | Twitter / X | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|
| **Hashtag value** | Low — algorithm penalizes 3+ tags | High — primary discovery signal |
| **Hashtags per post** | 0-1 (rarely 2) | 3-5 |
| **Format** | Inline only if natural; usually skip | End of post, after content + line break |
| **Discovery driver** | Replies, mentions, quote-tweets | Hashtags, follower feed, topic feeds |
| **Best growth lever** | Reply-guy presence in your niche | Long-form value posts with consistent hashtag set |
| **Cadence** | Multiple per day fine | 3-5 per week max — over-posting kills reach |
| **When NOT to use hashtags** | Most of the time on Twitter | Personal/casual posts; very intimate stories |
| **Mentions vs hashtags** | Mentions >> hashtags | Both work; mentions for reach, hashtags for topic discovery |
| **Lifespan of a post** | 30-90 minutes peak, then dead | 24-72 hours, with hashtag-driven long tail |

## Lumenari-specific tag candidates (push for adoption — sparingly)

These don't exist as established hashtags yet. The bet is that if Lumenari grows, owning the tag becomes valuable. The risk is hashtag inflation — using a tag nobody else uses just clutters your post.

**Use cadence: 1 of these per post, max. Never all three.**

| Tag | What it claims | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `#LumenariSkillKit` | Brand-owned tag for kit-specific posts | On kit launch tweets, kit deep dives, kit case studies. Use for ~3 months to seed. |
| `#SkillMD` | Generic-but-Lumenari-pioneered format tag | On posts about the SKILL.md format itself, format comparisons, format adoption news. Use when arguing the broader market case for the format. |
| `#LumenariAPI` | API-specific tag for integrator content | On API announcements, integrator stories, dev-facing API tutorials. Use on dev-flavored posts only. |

**The honest caveat about proprietary tags:**

Hashtag inflation is a real risk. A tag with 0 other users looks weird and signals "the brand is talking to itself." Until each of these tags has at least 50-100 organic uses by other accounts, they're closer to a brand watermark than a discovery tool.

**The rule:** include them on Lumenari-authored posts where they make natural sense, but never as one of your "main" hashtags on LinkedIn. Treat them as bonus, not budget.

**Stop using a tag if after 90 days it has fewer than 25 unique non-Lumenari users.** That's the sign that it isn't catching on, and continuing to use it is just signal pollution.

## Quick-reference checklist

**Before posting on Twitter:**
- [ ] Is there a real reason for a hashtag here? (Default: no)
- [ ] If yes, max 1 — and it's one from the table above
- [ ] Did I mention anyone who'd add value to the conversation?
- [ ] Is there a reply-guy opportunity I'm missing today?

**Before posting on LinkedIn:**
- [ ] 3-5 hashtags chosen from the post-type mapping
- [ ] Hashtags placed at the end after a line break, not inline
- [ ] At least 1 broad tag (`#AI`) and 1 niche tag (audience-specific)
- [ ] Optional: 1 Lumenari proprietary tag if it fits naturally
- [ ] Total hashtag count ≤ 5
