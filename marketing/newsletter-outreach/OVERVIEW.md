# Newsletter Outreach Pack — Lumenari

## The play

This is not a sponsorship pitch. It's a tip-off. Newsletter operators live in a constant state of "what should I cover next week" — and the ones whose audiences would actually use Lumenari (PMs, builders, AI-curious operators, indie hackers) are the ones who'll thank you for handing them a clean, novel item to drop in a roundup. The framing is "thought you'd want to know this exists" — not "would you take $500 to mention us." That distinction matters. Operators who write personality-driven newsletters can smell a paid pitch from three subject lines away, and they ignore them. They open emails from real founders who built a real thing and are giving them first look.

The mechanic is simple: a brief, personalized email referencing something recent they wrote, a two-line intro to Lumenari, a one-line "here's what's interesting for your readers" framing, and a soft CTA — "include in your roundup if it fits, no pressure either way." If even 3 of 20 pick it up, that's a meaningful traffic event for a launching product. The downside is zero — these are warm, considered notes from a builder, not spam. Worst case, no reply. Best case, a feature in front of 100K-700K engaged readers.

## Subject line variants

1. "Built a thing — thought it might fit your roundup"
2. "Tip for [Newsletter Name] — new AI catalog launched"
3. "Quick one for your readers, no pitch"
4. "Found something I think your audience would actually use"
5. "100 SKILL.md kits for Claude/ChatGPT — heads-up if useful"

Use #1 or #3 for the high-fit operators (Ben, Zain, Rowan). Use #2 for the analytical ones (Lenny, Sahar). Avoid #5 unless the operator is technical — it leads with format jargon.

## What to attach

- Link to **lumenari.io** (homepage, recommendation wizard is the sticky surface)
- Link to **lumenari.io/api** if they're a technical newsletter (TLDR AI, Last Week in AI, MarkTechPost) — public REST API is a story angle
- Press-kit boilerplate (1 sentence): *"Lumenari is a curated catalog of AI skill kits — drop-in prompt packs and SKILL.md bundles for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Gemini. 100 kits launched, priced $14-$249. Free recommendation wizard."*
- Two stat-worthy numbers if available by send time:
  - **100 kits** across 13 bundles, 4 formats each (SKILL.md, Custom GPT, optimization-pack, memory.md, quick-start)
  - **5 AI platforms supported** (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini) — multi-AI portability is the differentiator

Reference the press kit at `/marketing/press-kit/` if any operator asks for screenshots, founder photo, or longer boilerplate.

## Follow-up rule

**One follow-up at 7 days. Then stop.**

The follow-up is two sentences max: "Just bumping this in case it got buried — no worries if it's not a fit, won't follow up again." That's it. Operators respect a clean exit. A second follow-up converts no one and burns the relationship for the next product.

## Do / don't

**Do:**
1. Reference a specific recent issue or post of theirs in line one
2. Keep the email under 120 words — shorter is better
3. Sign as Chris, real human, with a one-line founder credibility note ("solo founder, Calgary")
4. Send from a real address (sovren.inc@gmail.com or chris@lumenari.io if set up by then)
5. Use UTM tags so you can attribute traffic if they include it

**Don't:**
1. Don't offer money — it changes the relationship and most won't reply
2. Don't attach files — link only
3. Don't send to a generic info@ address — find the operator's personal email
4. Don't follow up more than once
5. Don't BCC them on a mass send — every email is one-to-one or it doesn't work

## Tracking

- **UTM tags per newsletter:** `?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=[slug]` — e.g. `utm_content=bens-bites`
- **Log in a simple sheet:** date sent, operator email, subject line used, opened (if you can detect), replied, mentioned (yes/no/when), traffic delivered (from PostHog/GA UTM)
- **Success metric:** 3+ inclusions out of 20 = good run. 5+ = great run. 1 inclusion from a top-5 (Lenny, Ben, Rowan, Zain) = launch-defining event.
- **Track for 30 days** post-send — some operators sit on items for weeks before using them.

## What to do if they reply

**Path 1 — they're interested, want more info:**
Reply within 4 hours. Send the press-kit one-pager, 2 screenshots (homepage + a kit detail page), and offer a 15-min call if they want it. Don't oversell — they're already leaning in.

**Path 2 — they're interested but want a paid placement:**
Politely decline the paid route ("appreciate it — I'd rather earn the mention"), and ask if there's an organic fit. If not, thank them and move on. Don't argue. Don't negotiate. The product needs to stand on its own merit at this stage.

**Path 3 — they say no, not a fit:**
Reply with one line of thanks and a "would love to send you the next thing if you're open to it." Keep the door open. Newsletter operators have long memories for the polite ones.
