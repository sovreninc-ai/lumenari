## General sending rules

- **Personalize the first sentence.** Every cold and warm email must reference something they actually published in the last 90 days. If you can't find one, they're not the right partner — skip.
- **One ask per email.** The ask is always "want a free Pro+ login to evaluate" — never "sign up now." Lower the activation energy.
- **No follow-up for 5 business days.** Then one polite bump. Then close the file. Two no-replies means no.
- **Track every send.** Date, channel, variant used, response, status. The pipeline doc is the source of truth.
- **No mass send.** These are individual sends from Chris's actual inbox. Mailmerge tools detected and killed by spam filters and by the recipient's BS detector.
