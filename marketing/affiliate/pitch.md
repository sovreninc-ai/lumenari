## Design notes

**Palette:**
- Primary gradient: warm gold (`#E8B85A` → `#D4933A`) sweeping into deep blue (`#1B2A4E` → `#0E1A33`)
- Background: off-white (`#FAF7F0`) for content slides, deep blue for hero/CTA slides
- Accent: pure white for text on dark, deep blue for text on light, warm gold for emphasis
- Avoid pure black — use deepest blue instead

**Typography:**
- Headings: a confident serif (Tiempos Headline, Canela, or Recoleta) — establishes "curated" rather than "AI hype"
- Body: a humanist sans (Inter, IBM Plex Sans) — readable and modern
- Numbers / KPIs: monospace (IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono) — adds the "tools, not magic" feel
- Avoid Helvetica, Arial, or anything that screams generic SaaS

**Layout principles:**
- One idea per slide. If a slide needs sub-bullets, it's two slides.
- Generous margins. Lumenari is a curated catalog — the deck should feel curated too.
- No stock photography of laptops, hands typing, or sunrise-over-mountains. Use real product screenshots, abstract gradients, or nothing.
- Numbers are visual elements. Treat KPIs as typography, not chart labels.
- The CTA slide is the only place the URL appears at full size. Don't repeat it across slides.

**What to avoid:**
- Emojis in headlines
- Multiple gradients on the same slide
- "Passive income" language anywhere
- Comparison tables vs. other affiliate programs (looks defensive)
- Testimonial slides until we have real ones to put up
