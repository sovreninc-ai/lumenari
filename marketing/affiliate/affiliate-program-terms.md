## 1. Program overview

Lumenari is a curated catalog of AI skill kits, bundles, and the Pro+ subscription, sold at lumenari.io. The Lumenari Affiliate Program ("the Program") allows approved partners ("Affiliates") to earn commission by referring paying customers via a unique tracking link.

The Program is open to:
- Individual creators with an established audience (newsletter, social, podcast, YouTube, blog)
- Companies, agencies, and content publishers whose audience overlaps with Lumenari's customer base
- Educators, coaches, and consultants who serve professional audiences

The Program is **not** open to:
- Anonymous accounts with no demonstrable audience
- Operators of cashback, rebate, coupon-aggregator, or incentivized-traffic sites
- Anyone who has previously violated the Lumenari Terms of Service or this Affiliate Agreement
- Direct competitors of Lumenari (curated AI skill catalogs or substantially similar offerings)

## 2. Commission structure

| Product type | Commission rate | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Single skill kit ($14-$19) | 30% of net sale | One-time per purchase |
| Bundle ($35-$249) | 30% of net sale | One-time per purchase |
| Pro+ subscription (monthly) | 30% of each recurring payment | Up to 24 months from first payment |
| Pro+ subscription (annual) | 30% of each annual payment | Up to 24 months (i.e., 2 annual cycles) |
| Pro+ subscription (lifetime) | 30% of the one-time payment | One-time |
| Enterprise / API contract | 10% of first month's contract value | One-time, capped at $2,000 USD per deal |

**Recurring window for one-off purchases:** Affiliates earn 30% on **every** purchase a referred customer makes within **12 months** of their first purchase, not just the initial transaction.

**"Net sale"** means gross sale price minus refunds, chargebacks, applicable taxes, and Stripe processing fees deducted before commission calculation.

**Currency:** Commissions are calculated in the currency of the original transaction and paid to the Affiliate in their Stripe Connect default currency. Currency conversion uses Stripe's prevailing rate at the time of payout.

## 3. Cookie window and attribution

- **Cookie window:** 60 days from the most recent click on an Affiliate's tracking link.
- **Attribution model:** Last-click. The most recent Affiliate link clicked by the customer (within the 60-day window) receives credit for the resulting sale.
- **Cross-device:** Tracking persists where the customer signs in to a Lumenari account. Logged-out cross-device behavior may not be attributed.
- **Disputed attribution:** Where two or more Affiliates have valid claims to the same sale, Lumenari will review server-side click logs and award commission based on the timestamped last-click record. Lumenari's determination is final.
- **Self-referral:** Affiliates may not earn commission on their own purchases. Discounts for personal use are available separately on request.

## 4. Payout schedule

- **Frequency:** Monthly.
- **Terms:** NET-30 from the end of the calendar month in which the commission was earned. (Example: commissions earned in March are paid by April 30.)
- **Method:** Stripe Connect. Affiliates must maintain a Stripe Connect Express or Standard account in good standing.
- **Minimum threshold:** $50 USD equivalent. Balances below the threshold roll forward to the next month and continue rolling until the threshold is met.
- **Refund clawback:** If a referred customer is refunded after commission has been paid, the refunded commission amount is deducted from the Affiliate's next payout. If the deducted amount exceeds the next payout, the negative balance carries forward.
- **Inactive accounts:** Affiliate accounts with zero earned commission for 12 consecutive months may be deactivated; any unpaid balance below the $50 threshold may be forfeited at Lumenari's discretion.

## FAQ

### When do I get paid?
Monthly, NET-30 from the end of the calendar month. Commission earned in March is paid by April 30, via Stripe Connect, once your balance is at least $50 USD equivalent.

### How do I track my referrals?
Real-time dashboard at **lumenari.io/affiliate** once you're approved. Shows clicks, conversions, earnings, payout status, and per-link performance. Refreshed continuously.

### Can I promote on YouTube?
Yes — YouTube videos are explicitly permitted. Make sure the video description includes a clear affiliate disclosure (FTC requirement for US-based creators). We'll send you swipe scripts and B-roll suggestions if helpful.

### What if my referral upgrades to Pro+?
You earn 30% on the upgrade and on every recurring payment for up to 24 months from the first Pro+ payment. Upgrades are tracked automatically — you don't need to do anything to claim them, as long as the original purchase was attributed to you within the 60-day cookie window.

### What if my referred customer cancels and re-subscribes later?
If the cancellation-to-resubscribe gap is more than 60 days, the resubscribe is treated as a new sale and requires fresh attribution (a new click on your link within the 60-day window). If the gap is shorter, the original attribution may carry over — reach out and we'll review case-by-case.

### Can I run Google Ads pointing to my affiliate link?
Paid ads require **prior written approval from Lumenari per campaign**. **Brand bidding** — bidding on "Lumenari" or close variants — is **prohibited** and will result in immediate termination. Generic-keyword paid traffic (e.g., "AI prompt kits for real estate") is reviewed case-by-case.

### What happens if a customer requests a refund after I've been paid commission?
The refunded commission is deducted from your next payout. If the deduction is larger than your next payout, the negative balance rolls forward until cleared. We use the Stripe webhook for `charge.refunded` to handle this automatically.

### Can I be removed from the program?
Yes — Lumenari can terminate any Affiliate at any time for violation of these terms or for damage to the brand. Legitimate commissions earned before termination are still paid (except in fraud cases). See Section 9.
