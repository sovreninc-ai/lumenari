## Gallery order recommendation

1. **Slide 1 (Hero)** first — non-negotiable. Sets the brand and the visual register before any UI.
2. **Slide 3 (Kit detail with 4 formats)** second — the differentiator goes early. The 4-format multi-AI angle is what makes Lumenari interesting. Don't bury it.
3. **Slide 2 (Wizard)** third — once the value prop is clear, show the easiest entry point (free, no signup).
4. **Slide 5 (Pro+)** fourth — by now the visitor knows what's on offer; show the bundled deal.
5. **Slide 4 (API)** fifth — the credibility closer. "And there's an API" is the line that converts the technical PH audience.

Why this order beats the obvious "hero → wizard → kit → Pro → API" flow: the wizard is a tool, not a product. Showing the kit detail page second answers "what am I actually buying?" before showing how to find one. People skip products they don't understand, no matter how nice the wizard looks.
