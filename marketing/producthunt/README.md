# Lumenari — ProductHunt Launch Kit

Everything I need to ship the Lumenari launch on ProductHunt without scrambling on launch morning. Read this first, then work the checklists in order.

---

## Why ProductHunt for Lumenari

ProductHunt is the right venue because Lumenari sits exactly where the PH audience lives — builders, indie hackers, technical founders, and AI-curious operators who already use Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Codex daily. They don't need to be sold on AI. They need to be shown a better shape for the work they're already doing. The wizard, the multi-AI portability, and the SKILL.md format pioneer angle are all PH-friendly hooks.

It's also the right venue commercially. A solid PH launch doesn't replace a sales pipeline, but it does give Lumenari one credible day of distribution, ~5,000–15,000 site visits if it places top-5, and a permanent badge that pays back in trust signals on the homepage for years. The downside risk is small. Worst case, I get a bad day and learn what the messaging needs. Best case, it seeds the first 500 paying users.

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Launching on the wrong day.** Tuesday or Wednesday. Not Monday (algorithm penalty for being too eager). Not Friday (weekend kills momentum).
2. **Spamming the upvote ask.** "Please upvote" in DMs gets ignored and shadowbanned. Frame every ask as "thought you'd want to know" + "feedback welcome." See `outreach-message.md`.
3. **Disappearing after launch.** Most founders post the launch and ghost. Respond to every comment within 30 minutes for the first 6 hours. The PH algorithm weights comment velocity heavily.
4. **Generic first comment.** "Hey PH! Excited to launch X today!" is dead on arrival. The first comment is the hero of the page. See the 5 variants in `comment-templates.md`.
5. **Treating the launch as the goal.** It isn't. The goal is 500 paying users, not 500 upvotes. If a comment gives me a real product insight, I act on it within the week — that's the actual ROI of the day.
