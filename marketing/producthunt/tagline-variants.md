# Tagline Variants — Lumenari ProductHunt Launch

ProductHunt taglines max at 60 characters. Below are 8 candidates ranked, with the current working candidate at #1. Locking the tagline by T-7.

---

## Ranked candidates

### 1. Curated AI skill kits for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor & Gemini
**Characters: 60/60**
This is the current working candidate. It does the job — names the format (skill kits), the curation positioning, and the multi-AI portability in one line. Maxes the character budget but reads cleanly. The risk: "skill kits" is unfamiliar terminology for non-Anthropic users, and the platform list eats half the line.

### 2. The catalog of AI skill kits for the work you actually do
**Characters: 56/60**
Outcome angle. Sets up the contrast — generic prompts vs. a kit that fits your job. Removes the platform list, which sharpens the read but loses the multi-AI signal. Strongest if the gallery slide #1 carries the platform logos visually.

### 3. Stop fighting your AI. Start with the right skill kit.
**Characters: 53/60**
Pain-point angle. Two sentences, command-imperative. Strongest at converting cold scrollers because it leads with the felt problem (fighting AI) before the solution. Risk: slightly aggressive for the Apple-clean voice, and "right" is doing a lot of work.

### 4. 100 AI skill kits. One catalog. Every major AI.
**Characters: 49/60**
Multi-AI angle, structured rhythm. Three short clauses scan fast on a busy PH page. The number gives concreteness. Loses the "what is a skill kit" answer — anyone who doesn't know the term has to click to find out.

### 5. The SKILL.md catalog for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor & more
**Characters: 54/60**
SKILL.md angle — bets on the standard becoming familiar to the PH audience. Strong with technical builders who already know SKILL.md from Anthropic's announcement. Risk: it still feels insider. Most PH users haven't read the Anthropic post.

### 6. AI prompt libraries are dead. Skill kits are next.
**Characters: 49/60**
Contrarian angle. Strongest hook for the comment section — invites debate. Worst for the algorithm if the claim feels overreaching. Use this only if I want to set up a debate-driven first-comment strategy.

### 7. AI skill kits, four formats, one catalog.
**Characters: 41/60**
Simple/clean angle. Apple-style restraint. Trades information for breathing room. Works only if the gallery slide #1 instantly answers "what's a skill kit." Most likely to be remembered, least likely to convert cold.

### 8. Real skill kits for builders who use AI to ship.
**Characters: 49/60**
Builder angle. Filters for the audience that actually pays — operators and developers who use AI as a tool, not a toy. Loses the multi-AI signal but gains positioning clarity. Strong if Lumenari narrows to dev/builder kits over time; weaker if the 100-kit breadth is the moat.

## Recommendation

| # | Tagline | Clarity | Pain | Uniqueness |
|---|---------|---------|------|------------|
| 1 | Curated AI skill kits for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor & Gemini | High | Low | Medium |
| 2 | The catalog of AI skill kits for the work you actually do | Medium | Medium | High |
| 3 | Stop fighting your AI. Start with the right skill kit. | High | High | Medium |

**Pick #1 if** the gallery slides do the heavy lifting on what a skill kit is and why it matters. The platform names alone trigger recognition.

**Pick #2 if** the goal is to pull non-technical operators into the wizard. Outcome-led wins on cold traffic.

**Pick #3 if** the launch comment strategy is pain-led ("Most working pros reach for AI on the wrong task"). The tagline + comment compound.

**My current bet:** ship #1. The platform names are the most valuable real estate on a 60-character line — they make the multi-AI bet visible at thumbnail size, and they signal seriousness to the PH audience that already uses 2+ AI tools daily.
