# Outreach Messages — 24h Before Launch

DM templates for the 60-100 names on the supporter list. Send these 18-24 hours before launch — earlier and they forget, later and they miss the window. No mass-blast — every message gets the recipient's name and one personal sentence at the top.

---

## Variant A — Close friends in the indie hacker scene (~80 words)

Hey [name],

Launching Lumenari on ProductHunt tomorrow (Tue/Wed) — the AI skill kit catalog I've been building between pipefitter shifts. 100 kits, 4 formats per kit, works in Claude / ChatGPT / Cursor / Gemini.

If you're around at 12:01am PT or in the morning, an upvote would help — but more useful is an honest comment. The PH algorithm cares about comments way more than votes, and your read on it carries weight.

Link drops at midnight. I'll DM it the moment it's live.

Thanks Chris

## Variant B — Supportive customers / users (~100 words)

Hey [name],

Wanted to give you a heads-up because you've been one of the most useful early voices on Lumenari — tomorrow's the ProductHunt launch.

Going live at 12:01am PT (Tue/Wed). The link will be live on the homepage and I'll DM it the second it's up.

The most useful thing you can do isn't the upvote — it's leaving a real comment about what's worked for you. Even if it's a critique. The PH crowd reads the comment thread harder than they look at the vote count, and your perspective as someone who's actually used a kit cuts through.

No worries if you can't get to it. Either way, thank you for the past few months.

Chris

## Email subject line (if DM not available)

**Use:** "Launching tomorrow — wanted you to know first"

Reasoning: it's not asking for anything in the subject. It signals respect (you're hearing first) and creates a tiny obligation to open. Avoid "Please support my launch" — that's the inbox graveyard.

---

## What NOT to write

These five phrases will get the message ignored, screenshotted, or unsubscribed-from:

1. **"Please upvote me on ProductHunt!"** — instant ignore. The ask is naked, the value to the recipient is zero, and it reads like every other launch DM in their inbox that morning.

2. **"It would mean so much if you could support my launch"** — emotional leverage on someone who owes you nothing. Even close friends find this annoying. The version that works: "Wanted you to see this because you've been useful on it."

3. **"Just need 30 seconds of your time"** — false economy. Asking for time is the most expensive ask there is. Don't pretend it's small.

4. **"Hope you're well — quick favour"** — the corporate-speak opener immediately signals "this is a templated mass blast." Lead with the news, not the throat-clearing.

5. **"I'd really appreciate any support — likes, comments, shares, anything!"** — laundry list of asks. Pick one ask per message. The PH comment is worth 10x the share, so just ask for the comment.
