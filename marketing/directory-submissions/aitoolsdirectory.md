**Recommendation for Lumenari:** Start free. AIToolsDirectory has a fast, generous free tier and the directory's SEO is solid. Skip paid unless you see strong free-tier conversion or a launch moment that justifies the spend.

Lumenari is the catalog for that gap — sales, real estate, developer, marketing, and operations workflows you can use today.
