## Free vs paid tier

**Free tier**
- Standard listing with logo, description, screenshots, single primary category + tags.
- Light editorial review: typically 3-7 days.
- Listings appear in their newsletter "new this week" rotation if approved during the right window.

**Paid tiers**
- **Featured spot:** $25-$59 — homepage placement for 14-30 days.
- **Newsletter mention:** $99-$249 — list of ~25-40K AI enthusiasts.
- **Sponsored review post:** $200-$500 — written review on their blog.

**Recommendation for Lumenari:** Free. AI Tools Club is community-flavored and the audience is more enthusiast than buyer — paid placements rarely justify the spend for a tool like Lumenari. The free listing + the chance of being in their "new this week" newsletter rotation is enough.

### Description — 200 words

Lumenari is a curated catalog of 100 AI skill kits and 13 bundles for the people who already use Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, or Gemini and don't want to spend their evenings rewriting prompts.

Every kit ships in four installable formats — a Claude SKILL.md, a Custom GPT instruction set, a portable optimization-pack, and a memory.md — so you install once and the workflow follows you across whichever AI you're using. The free recommendation wizard surfaces the right kit in roughly 30 seconds based on your role and the outcome you want.

Pricing is direct: kits are $14-$19, bundles run $35-$249, a Pro+ subscription unlocks the full library plus updates, and a public REST API ($99 / $249 / $499 per month, with a free rate-limited tier) lets developers embed Lumenari kits inside their own products.

Built solo by Chris Holwell (Sovren Inc., Calgary AB) on a thesis that as foundation model capability converges, the *workflows* on top of those models become the durable layer. Lumenari is the catalog for that gap — sales, real estate, developer, marketing, writing, and operations workflows ready to install today.
