## Free vs paid tier

**Free tier**
- Basic listing in the catalog with logo, description, link, and category tags.
- Manual review queue: typically 1-2 weeks, occasionally up to 4 weeks during high submission volume.
- No "verified" badge, no homepage placement, no pinned ranking inside category.

**Paid tiers (TAAFT calls these "Pro" / "Featured")**
- **Skip the queue:** $79 one-time — review within 24-48 hours.
- **Verified badge:** ~$249/year — adds a checkmark, signals legitimacy, modest CTR lift.
- **Featured placement:** $300-$1,500/month depending on category competitiveness — pinned to the top of the chosen category page.
- **Sponsored "Tool of the Day" / newsletter slot:** $1,500-$5,000 — high-volume blast to ~500K subscribers.

**Recommendation for Lumenari:** Submit free. TAAFT is the highest-traffic AI directory, so even an unfeatured listing can produce 50-200 visits/month once indexed. Only consider paid upgrade if (a) the free listing converts well in PostHog and (b) you have a campaign moment (new bundle launch, big PR mention) that justifies a featured slot.
