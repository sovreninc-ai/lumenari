### Description — 200 words

Lumenari is a catalog of 100 AI skill kits and 13 bundles for the people who already use Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, or Gemini and don't want to keep rewriting prompts every time the underlying model changes.

Every kit ships in four installable formats — a Claude SKILL.md, a Custom GPT instruction set, a portable optimization-pack, and a memory.md — so you install once and the workflow follows you across whichever AI you're paying for. The free recommendation wizard surfaces the right kit in roughly 30 seconds based on your role and the outcome you want.

Pricing is direct: kits are $14-$19, bundles run $35-$249, a Pro+ subscription unlocks the full library plus updates, and a public REST API ($99 / $249 / $499 per month, with a free rate-limited tier) lets developers embed Lumenari kits inside their own products.

Built solo by Chris Holwell (Sovren Inc., Calgary AB) on the thesis that as foundation model capability converges, the *workflows* on top of those models become the durable layer. Lumenari is the catalog for that gap — sales, real estate, developer, marketing, writing, and operations workflows ready to install today.
