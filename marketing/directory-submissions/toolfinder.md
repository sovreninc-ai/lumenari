## Free vs paid tier

**Free tier**
- Standard listing with logo, description, screenshots, multi-category support.
- Editorial review: typically 1-2 weeks.
- Listings are hand-curated rather than auto-approved — passing review carries weight.

**Paid tiers**
- **Featured spot:** $99 one-time — homepage and category-page placement for 30 days.
- **"Editor's Pick" badge:** $199 — earned through review, not bought, but you can pay for promotion of an existing pick.
- **Newsletter mention:** $300-$800 — smaller list (~30K) but high engagement.
- **Annual partnership:** custom — content collabs, recurring placements.

**Recommendation for Lumenari:** Submit free. ToolFinder's audience is curated and engaged — visitors are typically purchase-intent. The free listing is enough; paid upgrade only makes sense if you have something newsworthy (new bundle launch, big customer story).

### Description — 200 words

Lumenari is a hand-curated catalog of 100 AI skill kits and 13 bundles, designed for the people who already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, or Gemini and don't want to spend their evenings rewriting prompts.

Every kit ships in four installable formats — a Claude SKILL.md, a Custom GPT instruction set, a portable optimization-pack, and a memory.md — so you install once and the workflow follows you across whichever AI you're using that quarter. Built solo by Chris Holwell (Sovren Inc., Calgary AB) on a bet that as model capability converges, the *workflows* on top become the durable layer. Lumenari is the catalog for that gap — covering sales, real estate, developer, marketing, writing, and operations workflows you can install today.
