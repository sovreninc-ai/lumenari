## Free vs paid tier

**Free tier**
- Standard listing with logo, description, screenshots, multi-category.
- Mostly automated review with light editorial pass: typically 2-5 days.
- Listings index quickly in Google.

**Paid tiers**
- **Featured listing:** $19-$49 — homepage spot for 14-30 days.
- **Verified badge:** $49/year — adds a checkmark.
- **Priority review:** $9 — same-day approval.

**Recommendation for Lumenari:** Free tier is fine. AIToolsHub is small but the free listing indexes well and the directory's submissions are fast. The $9 priority review isn't unreasonable if you want to lock the listing in for SEO purposes quickly, but otherwise skip.

## Submission notes

- **AIToolsHub auto-publishes** most listings after a quick admin pass. Get your assets right the first time — they don't email you to fix issues, they just reject silently.
- **Logo must be square.** SVG works but PNG 256x256 is safer here.
- **They highlight "new" submissions** for the first 7 days on the homepage — front-load any social promotion in that window.
- **They allow you to embed a YouTube video** in the listing — use the same 60-90 sec demo if you have it.
