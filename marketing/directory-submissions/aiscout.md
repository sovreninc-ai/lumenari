**Paid tiers**

- **"Approval bonus" promo:** AIScout occasionally runs a promo where any paid submission gets bumped editorial review + a tweet from their account — watch for it.

**Recommendation for Lumenari:** Submit free first. AIScout's editorial review is genuinely useful — they reject low-effort tools, so being approved carries weight. If they reject and ask for revisions, fix and resubmit rather than paying.

## Submission notes

- **AIScout's editorial review is real.** They will reject tools that look thin, have broken links, or have generic descriptions. Make sure every link on lumenari.io works before submitting.
- **They favor "differentiator" framing.** The four-format pitch is your hook — put it in the first sentence of the description.
- **They run "Scout Picks" lists** monthly — getting in early increases the chance of being included.
- **They have a community Slack** — joining and being a real participant for a few weeks before submitting can help.
- **They publish founder spotlights.** Once approved, ask if they'd run a 200-word founder note on Chris.
