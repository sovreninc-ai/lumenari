Only upgrade to a $49 featured slot if you have a launch moment (e.g. a new bundle drop or a press hit) where the 30 days of category-top placement compounds with other traffic sources.

## Submission notes

- **Futurepedia strongly prefers a video demo.** A 60-90 second YouTube unlisted video of the wizard flow → kit install → result will get you reviewed faster and ranked higher. Skip this and you risk being downranked vs. comparable tools.
- **They reject low-quality screenshots.** Use clean PNGs at exactly 1920x1080, no browser chrome unless it's intentional.
- **They like specifics in descriptions** — "100 kits, 13 bundles, 4 formats, $14-$19" — over generic AI-marketing language.
- **The submission form has a "Why this tool?" free-text field.** Use it to explain the multi-AI distribution thesis in 2 sentences.
- **Once live**, you can request a "category change" via support email if you land in a wrong bucket.
